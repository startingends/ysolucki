## (a) Where kappa enters Lemma 18.1, and where its range [3/4, 1] is used

     with s_kappa = (1 + kappa)/2 and the licence beta* <= s_kappa, Lemma 4.9 gives L'/L(s_kappa + e + it, psi) << log(conductor)(3+|t|)^2
     (18.11); shifting a smoothed von Mangoldt sum to Re s = s_kappa + e gives each normalised prime slot P^(-1/2) sum_{p~P} psi(p)... << P^(kappa/2),
     hence |Q_omega|^2 <= C Z^(kappa z + eps) (18.12), "2 s_kappa - 1 = kappa". For kappa = 1 this is absolute counting, no licence.
     So kappa is NOT an independent moment exponent: it is the exponent of the trivial-by-definition bound for prime sums right of the
     supremum beta* of real parts of zeros. Square-root cancellation in prime sums is kappa = 0, not kappa = 1/2.

     L'/L is bounded right of beta* by the DEFINITION of the supremum (plus Lemma 4.9's Borel-Caratheodory). Nothing is fed back that is
     not already true by definition.
