## (a) STRUCTURE: a long sum over MODULI controlled by the BOTTOM of a spectrum (not a duality)
KNOWN, read (Dunn-Radziwill, Thm main, conditional on GRH over Q(omega)): sum over primes p = 1 mod 3 of S_p/(2 sqrt p) W(p/X)
~ ((2 pi)^(2/3)/(3 Gamma(2/3))) (int W(x) x^(-1/6) dx) X^(5/6)/log X, S_p the cubic Gauss sum. Mechanism (Patterson 1977-78; FROM
MEMORY): sum_c g~(c) N(c)^(-s) is (essentially) a Fourier coefficient of Kubota's cubic Eisenstein series; its rightmost pole, at
the point where theta_3 is the residue (s = 5/6 in this normalisation), gives the main term X^(5/6); everything else is spectral
(cuspidal and continuous spectrum of the cubic cover). Classical twin (FROM MEMORY): Linnik-Selberg, sum_{c<=C} S(m,n;c)/c << C^(2 theta + eps)
with theta = the largest exceptional spectral parameter (Kuznetsov).
THE SUM: moduli c with N(c) <= X, length X. THE FAMILY: the spectrum of the cubic cover of FIXED level, cut at spectral parameter
T = O(1) by the test function. H/D = T^2/X -> 0: the family is infinitely shorter than the sum, and the long sum is governed
EXACTLY (an identity, Kuznetsov) by its lowest spectral terms. This is not a duality in the sense of SHORT_FAMILY 2(d): nothing
long is exchanged for something short; a long side is matched to the bottom of a fixed spectrum. The exact part is ONE term:
the residual spectrum, i.e. theta_3.

## (b) NEAR SIDE: S(H, D) of SHORT_FAMILY.md at H = D^(1/2)
(b1) PROVED (from the paper's Step 3, exact for every H): S(H,D) <~ DH + (H/D) sum_{N(h) << calH} |B_h(D)|^2, calH ~ D^2/H = D^(3/2),
     B_h(D) = sum over squarefree primary n, N(n) ~ D, of alpha(n) gamma_2(n) xi(n) chi_n(h): a sum over MODULI n of normalised cubic
     Gauss sums, twisted by a character of conductor ~ N(h) (sextic reciprocity, FROM MEMORY) and by the angular factor alpha(n) = n/|n|.
(b2) KNOWN, read (11/12 paper, A.2): the twisted series T(s, Psi) built from these coefficients is ENTIRE, because "the horizontal
     derivative removes the constant mode" of theta. PROVED reading: the factor alpha, which the Moebius function itself
     produces (mu gamma_(-1) = chi_n(-1) G^(-1) alpha gamma_2), kills exactly the residual (theta) term that gives Patterson's X^(5/6).
     So in the structure of (a), the Moebius-dual sums are the sums with NO bottom-of-spectrum main term.
(b3) CONSISTENCY CHECK (PROVED, given (0c) of SHORT_FAMILY): under GRH, S(H,D) << H D^(1+eps) for every H. If the residual term were
     present, the rows h for which chi_.(h) is a cube twist (h a square up to sixth powers: about calH^(1/2) of them) would have
     |B_h| ~ D^(5/6), contributing (H/D) calH^(1/2) D^(5/3) = H^(1/2) D^(5/3) to S, which at H = D^(1/2) is D^(23/12) > HD = D^(3/2): the
     diagonal bound would be FALSE. So the vanishing in (b2) is forced by GRH: the Moebius side must be "cuspidal" in (a)'s sense.
     (The squares-count calH^(1/2) is HEURISTIC; the sign of the conclusion does not depend on it.)
(b4) WHAT (a) THEN GIVES at H = D^(1/2): the bound on B_h comes from the cuspidal and continuous spectrum of the cubic cover at
     LEVEL ~ h, whose Weyl-law size grows like N(h) >= D for most h: on the dual side the family is again longer than the sum.
     So with only the inputs I know (Heath-Brown's cubic large sieve, sharp by Dunn-Radziwill under GRH; Patterson-type
     bounds for fixed level), (a) gives NOTHING below the trivial S << D^2. Exponent: none.
     CONJECTURE (would suffice): sum_{N(n) ~ X} alpha(n) gamma_2(n) psi(n) << X^(1/2) (X N(cond psi))^eps for all ray class psi, i.e.
     Lindelof in the conductor aspect for the angular-weight cuspidal part. Then S(D^(1/2), D) << (H/D) calH D^(1+eps) = D^(3/2+eps):
     zero-free Re s > 3/4, and 17/24 with the sixth-power amplifier (SHORT_FAMILY 0b). This is a statement of Lindelof type,
     not weaker than GRH in strength for these twists: it is a reformulation unless (a)'s spectral side supplies it.

## (c) IMPOSTOR
First line false for the Davenport-Heilbronn data: (b1)'s identification of the dual coefficients as alpha gamma_2 (theta
coefficients). It needs mu(n) pointwise on squarefree n (gamma_2(p)^3 = -alpha(p): "the -1 is mu"), i.e. the Euler product in the form
"mu is a product of local Gauss-sum factors". SAME line as SHORT_FAMILY 4(b). New in this frame (RISK, an analogy): in (a) a
term of size X^(beta) in a long sum is an "exceptional eigenvalue" at the bottom of the spectrum. The impostor's Moebius-type
sums have the term D^(0.808) from the pole of 1/f; for an Euler product the only analogue would be a Siegel-type real zero or an
off-line zero. So "zero-free region for 1/L" <-> "no exceptional spectrum for the Moebius-dual sums", and for zeta_K the one
residual term that (a) knows (theta) is exactly the one the Moebius structure removes (b2).
