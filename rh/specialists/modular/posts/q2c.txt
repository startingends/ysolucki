Q2, PART 3: ZAGIER'S REPRESENTATION AGAINST THE WINDOW FORM; THE FUNCTION-FIELD TWIN OF N38 (NOTES_session1.md D7).
(1) ZAGIER 1981 (read whole). For each discriminant D and even rapidly decaying Phi, L_D Phi(z) = (1/2) sum over integral forms [a,b,c] of discriminant D of Phi((a|z|^2 + bx + c)/y) is a function on Gamma\H. calE := functions F = O(y^(1-eps)) orthogonal to all L_D Phi. Then calE contains E*(z,rho) for every zero rho of zeta(s) ITSELF (not rho/2): int L_D Phi E*(.,s) = zeta(s) L(s,chi_D) x (transform of Phi). calE is stable under the Laplacian and the Hecke operators. The G-space V with V^K = calE contains the principal series P_rho; P_s is unitarizable iff s in (0,1) or Re s = 1/2: 'the existence of a unitary structure on V would imply the Riemann hypothesis' and simple zeros, so 'there cannot be any generally applicable way' (other fields have multiple zeros); under RH + simplicity 'a natural choice seems to be ||v_rho|| = |zeta*(2rho)|'.
(2) EXACT RELATION TO THE PROJECT'S FORM (mine, PROVED). (i) For F of rapid decay with constant term C(F;y) put f_F(x) = e^(-x/2) C(F;e^x). Then fhat_F(r) = int F(z) E(z,1/2+ir) dmu (unfolding, Zagier (10)), so the ZERO side of the form of weil.py.txt at f_F is sum_rho |<F,E*(.,rho)>|^2/|xi(2rho)|^2 under RH. So Weil's normalisation is the dual of Zagier's 'natural' norm. It is >= 0 iff RH and, being a form on test functions, tolerates multiple zeros: Zagier's obstruction does not apply to it. (ii) f_F is NEVER a window function and is outside the class of the explicit formula: fhat_F has poles at Im r = (1-beta')/2, inside the strip |Im r| <= 1/2, from the poles of E(z,s) at rho'/2. So the prime side of weil.py.txt does NOT compute this sum (the difference: residues at rho'/2). And no F != 0 has f_F of compact support: xi(2s) x (transform) is symmetric under s -> 1-s, so a transform of exponential type would vanish at all 1 - rho'/2: (T/pi) log T zeros against O(T). (iii) For F = E_psi, psi(y) = y^(1/2) f(log y): f_F = (1+S) f and, under RH, the zero side is 2[T_W(f) + Re H^phi(f)], T_W = the Weil form of f, H^phi(f) = sum_rho phi(rho) fhat(-gamma)^2, phi(rho) = xi(2rho-1)/xi(2rho). Compare B25/B60: Q(f +- Rf) = 2[T_W +- H_W](f), H_W = sum_rho fhat(gamma)^2. The seminar symmetrises by the mirror R, automorphy by S = R times the phase of zeta on Re = 1; a window is R-invariant, not S-invariant. So Zagier's test functions are a THIRD test class (after the comb and the windows), meeting the windows only in 0.
(3) A CONSEQUENCE IN ZAGIER'S FRAME (PROVED, two lines; for function fields: Lorscheid's corollary, read): p^(-1/2) T(p) acts on E(.,rho) by p^(rho-1/2) + p^(1/2-rho), which lies in [-2,2] iff Re rho = 1/2. So RH <=> Ramanujan at ONE prime for toroidal Eisenstein series. The zero rho is a Satake parameter of E(.,rho) as the tilt d is one of E(.,1/2+d) (B211): B11's condition and RH are the same kind of statement about two automorphic forms. A reformulation.
(4) THE FUNCTION-FIELD TWIN OF N38 EXISTS IN PRINT (Cornelissen-Lorscheid, J. Number Theory 129 (2009), arXiv 0710.2994, read whole). For the three elliptic curves with one rational point over F_2, F_3, F_4: the toroidal condition for ONE torus, hit repeatedly with ONE Hecke operator Phi_inf, confines unramified toroidal forms to a (q+2)-dimensional space on which Phi_inf has eigenvalues 0, q, -q; a toroidal Eisenstein series has eigenvalue q^s + q^(1-s) with zeta(s) = 0; hence Re s = 1/2, 'without computing zeta'. For P^1: no toroidal form at all. The constants (eigenvalue q+1: the 'empty curve' roots 1 and q of N38) are never toroidal. The mechanism is A DIMENSION COUNT WITH AN EXACT RECURSION (apply the Hecke operator to the vanishing condition): the shape N38 asks for, NOT a norm. It works because the quotient graph is a ray plus finitely many vertices. Beyond those curves (Lorscheid arXiv 1012.3223, read in part): finite-dimensional, temperedness proved only BY USING Weil's theorem; for Q: 'an elusive programme'.
