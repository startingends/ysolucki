ANSWER TO B3 (exit E5: GL(2) and Eisenstein). Long form with proofs: rh/specialists/modular/S3_B3.md. Normalisation: lambda(m)lambda(n) = sum_{d|(m,n)} lambda(mn/d^2); A_f = sum mu(n) lambda_f(n) W(n/D).
(a) COEFFICIENTS: cusp f: lambda_f. Eisenstein E(.,1/2+it): lambda_t(n) = sum_{ab=n}(a/b)^(it), Kuznetsov weight ~ dt/|zeta(1+2it)|^2 (Zagier (14) read; the weight FROM MEMORY). Residual: the constant, lambda = sigma_1(n)/sqrt n (non-tempered; absent from Petersson/Kuznetsov). PROVED: NO Hecke eigen-system is an exact copy: lambda(p)^2 = lambda(p^2) + 1 forbids lambda(p) = lambda(p^2) = 1. The right notion is by L-function: sum mu(n) lambda(n) n^(-s) = G(s)/L(s,pi), G absolutely convergent for Re s > 1/2 (PROVED). So only the Eisenstein members' mu-sums see the zeros of zeta (at rho +- it).
(b) POSITIVE PROPORTION? (b1) The Eisenstein members ARE the t-family inside GL(2). In a spectral family of size H ~ T^2 their weight fraction is ~H^(-1/2) (Weyl law, FROM MEMORY): a 'delta = 1/2' by a different mechanism, but they are not copies of one sum. (b2) The copy direction has ZERO weight. The members nearest one sum (|t| << 1/log D, A_t ~ sum mu d W) weigh int t^2 dt ~ (log D)^(-3), since E(z,1/2) = 0 identically (phi(1/2) = -1), so the Plancherel density vanishes at t = 0 (PROVED). (b3) The ONE way a copy enters with full weight is Rankin-Selberg. Petersson averages |lambda_f(n)|^2 to 1 (FROM MEMORY). PROVED: lambda_f(n)^2 = sum_{d|n} lambda_f(d^2) for squarefree n, so A_f^RS := sum mu(n) lambda_f(n)^2 W = sum_d mu(d) lambda_f(d^2) sum_{(m,d)=1} mu(m) W(dm/D), whose d = 1 term is A_1 itself in EVERY member; equivalently zeta divides every member's L-function (G/(zeta L(sym^2 f))). Copies of density one, and (PROVED) circular three ways. First moment: N71's tautology. Diagonal second moment: RH-strength by itself (E3). General sieve with a_n = 1: Delta >= H D, like H copies (Theorem 1).
(c) FIRST LINES. Hecke family: the amplifier never starts (no copies). The spectral large sieve then holds WITHOUT a Theorem-1 penalty but bounds mu-sums of L(s,f), not zeta; the only zeta-carrying members are archimedean (the t-family), weight H^(-1/2), zero at the copy direction. So E5 = the t-family = S2's short-family / Nyman-Beurling wall (sums of length D >> T). Rankin-Selberg family: first line 'zeta divides every member's L-function' -> E3 circularity / N71 tautology. N49's 1 - delta/2: Hecke: no delta; Eisenstein count 1/2 but not copies; RS delta = 1 -> 1/2, circular.
RISK, proved in this form: a GL(n) Hecke family whose averaged |coefficients|^2 is the constant has zeta | L(pi x pi-bar) for every member: the pole at s = 1 IS that factor (Jacquet-Shalika, FROM MEMORY). So for automorphic families E5 closes at Landau's pole (N63). An exit would need an orthogonality whose diagonal is the trivial character WITHOUT a pole in each member's Rankin-Selberg square. I know no such family.
