ANSWER TO B22 (long form: rh/specialists/modular/S2_B22_B23.md, part B22).
(1) PROVED (paper's Step 3 run on one stratum): on u = w^3, (w^3/n)_6 = (w/n)_2, so A_(w^3) = sum mu(n)nu(n)(w/n)_2 W: Moebius twisted by QUADRATIC characters of K. Poisson in w gives QUADRATIC Gauss sums of modulus z_1 z_2.
(2) NO ABSORPTION (Gauss sign facts FROM MEMORY): the normalised quadratic Gauss sum satisfies gamma^(2)(z)^2 = (-1/z)_2 and is itself a ray-class character up to the reciprocity cocycle. So mu(z)gamma^(2)(z) is mu times a character: mu stays. The stratum is CLOSED under Poisson: (family H', length D) -> (family D^2/H', length D), coefficients unchanged. A pure duality.
(3) EXACT OBSTRUCTION: absorption needs (normalised Gauss sum)^m = -(character) at EVERY prime, so the primes of squarefree n multiply to (-1)^omega(n) = mu(n). m = 3: g^3 = p J with primary J = -pibar, so gamma_2^3 = -alpha ('the -1 is mu'; digest Lemma 4.1, checked by READER-4). m = 2: chi^2 is trivial, there is no Jacobi sum, g(chi)g(chibar) = chi(-1)p, and the sign is the CHARACTER value (-1/p), not the constant -1. In one line: a cubic Gauss sum cubed is a Hecke character times -1; a quadratic one squared is a Hecke character times +1.
(4) SAME OBSTRUCTION, structurally (PROVED from the coefficient formulas): the automorphic object behind order-m Gauss sums is the theta function of the m-fold cover. For m = 3 its coefficients are cubic Gauss sums spread over squarefree x cube indices (Patterson, read in Dunn-Radziwill (5.7)). For m = 2 it is sum_v e(v^2 z): coefficients = indicator of SQUARES, and the rows w = v^2 are exactly the copies u = v^6. So on this stratum the would-be absorber is supported on the trivial characters themselves: circular, and the large sieve is blind there (with the square rows: (H' + D H'^(1/2)) sum|a|^2).
(5) IF IT DID ABSORB (PROVED, N49's formula): inside the stratum the copies are the squares, density 1/2. A diagonal mean square at the stratum's self-dual point H' = D would give |A_1|^2 << D^2/D^(1/2), i.e. A_1 << D^(3/4): zero-free Re s > 3/4. That is exactly N49's cap, where FI and SP-sieve put parity. Goldmakher-Louvel's quadratic sieve gives that diagonal only over SQUAREFREE rows, i.e. without the copies.
(6) SHIMURA-WALDSPURGER (FROM MEMORY): half-integral weight coefficients are square roots of central values L(1/2, pi x chi_w): exact for L at the centre, nothing for 1/L. I know no exact formula for sum_w |A_(w^3)(D)|^2 at H^(1/3) < D and would not trust one built from it. It carries |L(1/2)|^2, the positive, impostor-shared kind of information.
