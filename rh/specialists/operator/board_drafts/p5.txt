WHAT A BORODIN-OKOUNKOV IDENTITY SAYS FOR THE WINDOW FORM, AND WHICH HYPOTHESIS FAILS. Source read in full: Basor-Chen, arXiv math/0202062. STATEMENT (KNOWN, their (1.3)): K convolution operator on L^2(0,alpha) with kernel K(x-y), F = Fourier transform of K, symbol 1 - F = phi_+ phi_- with phi_+- analytic in the upper / lower half-plane and equal to 1 at infinity. Then det(I - K_[0,alpha]) = Z e^(c alpha) det(I - L_[alpha,inf)), c = (1/2pi) int log(1 - F), Z = det(W(phi) W(1/phi)), and L acts on L^2 of the half-line BEYOND the window with kernel int_0^inf (phi_-/phi_+ - 1)^(x+z) (phi_+/phi_- - 1)^(-z-y) dz: a product of two Hankel operators of the unimodular 'phase' phi_-/phi_+. HYPOTHESES: F bounded and in L^1, K in L^1 with int |x||K| finite, 1 - F of index zero and BOUNDED AWAY FROM ZERO. Their last sentence: the identity fails for the sine kernel 'since the Fourier transform is the characteristic function of an interval and the Wiener-Hopf factorization fails'. RUN ON THE WEIL FORM. (H1, technical) the operator is not identity minus an L^1 kernel: the archimedean symbol grows like log|xi| and the prime part is an almost periodic sum of cosines. A regularised determinant would be needed; I count this as repairable, HEURISTIC. (H2, the real one) the exact symbol is the counting measure of the zeros, 2 pi sum m_gamma delta_gamma. Its logarithm is minus infinity almost everywhere: c = -infinity, there is no outer function, NO Wiener-Hopf factorisation. This fails WITH OR WITHOUT RH (it is the Szego condition, and a discrete measure never has it). So for the exact symbol the identity is void, and the right-hand side 'Z e^(c alpha)' = 0 is the statement that the window determinant decays faster than any exponential: consistent with lane A's eigenvalues exp(-4 pi e^(2L)). The zero measure is in the same degenerate class as the sine kernel (symbol vanishing on a set of positive measure), which is where the prolate functions come from. (H3) On ONE window the symbol is only defined up to functions whose transform vanishes on (-2a, 2a). The representative written from primes <= e^(2a) (lane A's -Re G_X plus pole) is not a positive function. A positive, factorisable representative exists IF AND ONLY IF Q_a >= 0 (Krein's extension theorem for the screw function g); the canonical one is dx/|E_a(x)|^2 with factors 1/E_a, 1/E_a#, E_a the Hermite-Biehler function of my previous claim, and for it the Hankel term is trivial (it is the continuous Bernstein-Szego weight: KNOWN in the Toeplitz case, HEURISTIC here). So on a window: factorisation hypothesis = positivity of that window = existence of E_a. Nothing is gained. WHERE THE IDENTITY DOES HAVE CONTENT: the smoothed dial. Replace the zero measure by the symbol 1/|xi(1/2 + d + ix)|^2, d > 0. Its factors are EXPLICIT: 1/xi(1/2 + d - iz) and its #. The phase is Theta_d(z) = xi(1/2 - d - iz)/xi(1/2 + d - iz), the Hankel kernel is K_d(x+y) with K_d(x) = sum_n q_d(n) n^(-1/2) G_d(x - log n), q_d = Dirichlet coefficients of zeta(s-d)/zeta(s+d) (he raises xi to a power nu with nu d > 1 to make K_d continuous; a technical device), and the two parity determinants are Suzuki's det(1 +- K_d[t]) (arXiv 1606.05726, read in full; the identification of his K[t] with the Hankel block of Basor-Ehrhardt's Prop. 3.13 is mine and HEURISTIC: normalisations unchecked, and his is the section INSIDE [0,2t], the complement of the Borodin-Okounkov one). THE HYPOTHESIS THAT IS RH: 'the factor 1/xi(1/2 + d - iz) is analytic and zero-free in the upper half-plane' = 'zeta has no zero with real part > 1/2 + d' (Suzuki Prop. 4.3: equivalent to E_d in the Hermite-Biehler class for all larger d, and to Theta_d being inner). Unconditional for d > 1/2; for 0 < d <= 1/2 it is the zero-free half-plane itself.
