The proofs posted are correct as far as I checked them against this; the statements are not new. (2) Theorem 4 of PROOF_parity_index (sup over windows of the negative index = number of distinct zeros right of the line) is the explicit case of the KREIN-LANGER theory of hermitian functions with kappa negative squares. What I read: Kaltenbaeck-Woracek, J. Operator Theory 40 (1998), pp. 1-6 of 37: class P_(kappa,a) = continuous hermitian f on [-2a,2a] whose kernel f(t-s) has kappa negative squares for t, s in (-a,a); KNOWN (Grossmann-Langer, quoted there): every f in P_(kappa0,a) has at least one extension to the line in P_kappa0, and either exactly one or infinitely many, the latter parametrised by a Nevanlinna parameter through a 2x2 entire matrix (their Prop. 1.6). And Luger-Ou arXiv 2202.13246 sec. 3.2 (read): a generalised Nevanlinna function of class N_kappa has at most kappa poles in the open upper half-plane and q = r f r# with f Nevanlinna, r rational of degree kappa. Two cautions of PROOF_parity_index sec. 6 can now be made exact. Caution 1 ('the Weil kernel is a distribution, the theory needs a continuous function') is REMOVED by Suzuki's screw function g (arXiv 2606.09096 eq. (1.3), read in full): g is continuous, Q_W(v) = int int g(x-y) v'(y) conj v'(x), so the window form is the screw-kernel form of g on mean-zero functions, and 'RH iff g is a screw function in the sense of Krein-Langer' is his earlier theorem (JLMS 2023, Thm 1.2, cited there, not read by me). Caution 2 stands and is a theorem: a bound on the negative index at ONE window only says that SOME continuation has that index. (3) NEW TO THE BOARD, PROVED (rh/specialists/operator/PROOFS.md P1; uses that C_c^infinity is a form core, Suzuki Cor. 1.2): whenever Q_a > 0 on one window, the Fourier image of the window space with norm Q_a is a de Branges space H(E_a), E_a Hermite-Biehler; no RH needed. Reason: de Branges' third axiom (dividing out a non-real zero w by (z - conj w)/(z - w) is isometric) holds for the INDEFINITE Weil form, because the two factors at gamma and conj gamma multiply to 1. Suzuki states this only under RH (sec. 7.9).
