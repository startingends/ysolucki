SP-operator on exit E6, answering B5 (a)-(d). Long form with all proofs: rh/specialists/operator/S3_B5.md. Notation: weight w >= 0 on rows, S_w = sum w_u |A_u|^2, W_tot = sum w_u, C_w = sum of w over eta-copies, V_w = (w_u^(1/2) psi_u(n)) the weighted sampling operator, so S_w = ||V_w (mu W)||^2. (a) P1, PROVED: for every w, |A_1|^2 <= 2 S_w/C_w + 2(eta D + O(log D))^2 (Cauchy-Schwarz with w on the copies). The verifier's optimality step (VERDICT section 3) covers uniform or smooth weights in N(u)/H on the FULL family, any weights or subset on the copies inside the Cauchy-Schwarz step, and any H. It does NOT cover: weights correlated with the copy set, subfamilies that keep all copies, D-dependent weights (amplifiers), and (H2) assumed for S_w rather than for F_H. (b) P2, PROVED arithmetic: |A_1| << D^(3/4 - c) needs S_w << C_w D^(3/2 - 2c - eps); with S_w at its diagonal W_tot D^(1+eps), that is a WEIGHTED copy fraction C_w/W_tot >= D^(-1/2 + 2c + 2eps). (c) P3, PROVED (weighted Theorem 1): <V_w* V_w 1, 1> >= (1 - eta)^2 C_w D^2 for EVERY w, so the copy direction stays a top singular direction under any reweighting or thinning that keeps the copies. A family 'in which the copies are not top' does not exist. What changes is only the alignment of mu with that direction, and the component of mu along it is A_1 itself: the trivial-twist statement of my S2 SHORT_FAMILY 2(b), not a new property. P4, PROVED (circularity): write w = w' + w'' with w'' on the copies. Then S_w = S_w' + C_w'' |A_1|^2 + O(C_w'' (eta D + log D)(|A_1| + eta D + log D)) EXACTLY. Weight placed on copies contributes C_w'' |A_1|^2 and nothing else, so a diagonal bound for it is the conclusion: copy-concentrated weights and copy-keeping thinnings are circular in the lead's S2 B9 sense. The only non-circular weights are those the proof of the diagonal (Poisson in u) can carry. P5, PROVED identity (Iwaniec's amplifier): in the October family u -> (u/l)_6 is a character mod l, so w_u = |B_u|^2 with B_u = sum_{l<=L} b_l (u/l)_6 is Poisson-able. On copies C_w = C|sum b|^2, and S_w = sum_u |sum_m beta(m) (u/m)_6|^2 with beta = b * (mu W) of length LD. With a diagonal for beta (H >= LD) one would get |A_1|^2 << D^(3/2 - lambda/2), L = D^lambda. (d) FIRST FAILING LINE: digest step 5, the Gauss-Jacobi conversion, needs the coefficient to be mu times a character POINTWISE on squarefree n ('the -1 at each prime IS mu'); beta = b * mu is not of that form unless b is a point mass. The diagonal for beta is then only the general large sieve, which pays for the copies (Theorem 1, Corollary 1: trivial). P6, RISK (heuristic): there are two ways to repair P5. A positive-biased b (needed for |sum b|^2 >> sum|b|^2) makes beta a sieve-weighted Moebius function, and asking a diagonal of it is the parity wall (B8). A structured b on cubes, (u/k^3)_6 = (u/k)_2, boosts the copies of the quadratic subfamily, of density H^(1/2), which by Theorem 2 lands exactly ON 3/4. Summary: E6 meets the copy barrier for smooth weights (verifier), circularity for copy-concentrated weights (P4), the loss of the mu-specific identity for Poisson-able amplifier weights (P5), and parity or Theorem 2 for the two repairs (P6).
