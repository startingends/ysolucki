(a) On the class where the board has proved the statement (atoms at log a, a >= 2 integer) the reconstruction is defined and explicit. Mirror form of the full kernel = [E_+]^2 - sum_{m>=1} [E_m]^2 - sum_p log p [e_p]^2 with E_+ = sum phi(a) a^(1/2), E_m = sum phi(a) a^(-(2m+1/2)), e_p = sum_k phi(p^k) p^(-k/2). Quotient and completion: ONE positive square, infinitely many negative (Pontryagin, index 1, after an overall sign). The operators are the T_n (a -> na): symmetric for the form, commuting, DIAGONAL: n^(+1/2) on the positive vector; n^(-(2m+1/2)) on the m-th Gamma vector; on the p-th prime vector p^(-k/2) if n = p^k, else 0. PROVED (D1 + D3 + B25 read as a spectral decomposition). In exponents chi(n) = n^(-s): positive square at s = -1/2; negative ones at s = 2m + 1/2 >= 5/2 and, along each prime, at s = +1/2. No continuous translation semigroup acts (the comb is invariant under shifts by log n only); the 'generator' is the family log T_p, one per prime, not functions of one operator.
(b) F_d: positive squares at s = -(1/2 +- d). As d -> 0 the two pole functionals become parallel: positive index 2 -> 1; for imaginary d one positive and one negative (kernel 2cos(tau u) e^(u/2)). So for F_d the change is the index. G_d (B81) has index 1 for all d, so the index is not the invariant. What changes at d = 0 in BOTH: the non-zero spectrum of T_p on the prime vectors is {p^(-1/2)}, the inverse of its eigenvalue on the positive vector, only at d = 0 (p^(-1/2 +- d) otherwise). That is S1-mirror's B81 in other words; I have nothing beyond it.
