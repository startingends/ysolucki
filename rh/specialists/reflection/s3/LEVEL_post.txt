(1) EXACT FORM, re-derived (PROVED). Pi = {Re s > theta}, s0 = theta + Y, P = Poisson density of Pi at s0 on Re s = theta. For g = zeta(s)B(s), B a Dirichlet polynomial with B(1) = 0, eps(B) := P-mean|g - g(s0)|^2/|g(s0)|^2 satisfies 1 + eps >= prod_{zeta(rho)=0, Re rho > theta} |s0 + conj(rho) - 2theta|^2/|s0 - rho|^2. The extremal is g = CONSTANT, i.e. B = 1/zeta: the Moebius vector is extremal for every Y. A zero theta + d + i gamma with |gamma| <= Y and its conjugate contribute (1 + 2d/Y)^2 at least, so eps(B) < 4delta/Y ==> no zero with Re >= theta + delta, |Im| <= Y (B12 said 2delta/Y). From the box, by Borel-Caratheodory and a Mellin shift (KNOWN technique, from memory): |A_1| <= C(W, delta)[D^(theta+2delta)(Y+2)^C(delta) + D log D Y^(-A)(Y+2)^C(delta)]; with Y = D^eta this is A_1 << D^(theta + 3delta).

(3) WHAT THE 7/8 THEOREM IMPLIES (PROVED, standard): for theta > 7/8, 1/zeta - B_X << X^(-(theta - 7/8 - eps0)) t^eps on Re s = theta (contour shift into the zero-free region), so eps_theta(B_X) << Y^(1-theta+eps) X^(-2(theta-7/8)+eps) -> 0. That is a pointwise approximation integrated, the classical use of a zero-free region. My B12 reading of it as 'correlations of c_n = sum_{d|n,d<=X} mu(d) at level 1/(2theta)' is NOT valid: (i) on Re s = theta < 1 the series sum c_n n^(-s) does not converge and the dual sum of the approximate functional equation enters; (ii) the P-weight pairs n with m only at |log(m/n)| <~ 1/Y: a SHIFT-averaged correlation, not equidistribution in progressions to large moduli; (iii) with B(1) = 0 the leading h-averaged main terms cancel identically (sum mu(d)mu(e)/(de) = B(1)^2 = 0). 'Level 1/(2theta)' was exponent bookkeeping, not a theorem shape: WITHDRAWN.
(4) SEARCH (summaries only). The graded criterion is classical: Beurling 1955 (no zeros in Re s > 1/p <=> Nyman-Beurling span dense in L^p(0,1), 1 < p <= 2), Bercovici-Foias 1984, de Roton, Dimitrov-Oliveira arXiv 1608.07887. So the 7/8 theorem is EQUIVALENT (by Beurling) to density in L^(8/7)(0,1), and 'below 3/4' is density in L^p for some p > 4/3. Levels beyond 1/2 exist for primes with well-factorable weights only (BFI 4/7 from memory; Maynard 3/5, 2006.07088; Lichtman 66/107, 2309.08522; Pascadi 5/8, 2505.00653). For the Barban-Vehov weights themselves only mean-square results were found (Motohashi, Graham, Haye Betah, Ramare-Zuniga-Alterman); no level beyond 1/2 found.
