DEFINITION: YES (PROVED). On T^infinity with Haar, chi -> chi(n) are distinct characters, hence orthonormal: E|sum_{n<=D} mu(n) n^(-1/2) chi(n)|^2 = sum_{n<=D} mu(n)^2/n for EVERY D. On the torus no family is shorter than the sum; the near side is the restriction to a finite piece of the Kronecker line (Kronecker-Weyl / Bohr-Jessen give it only as T -> inf; KNOWN, from memory).
(a) STRUCTURE: Bochner on the DISCRETE group Q_+^x (dual T^infinity) plus the gcd identity. A t-family nu >= 0 is, via log: Q_+^x -> R, the positive-definite function q -> g(log q), g = nu-hat (torus: g = delta). PROVED (checked to machine precision, N = 80): (*) int |sum_{n<=N} mu(n) n^(-1/2-it)|^2 dnu = sum over COPRIME a,b <= N of g(log a/b) mu(a)mu(b)(ab)^(-1/2) S_ab(N/max(a,b)), S_r(x) = sum_{k<=x,(k,r)=1} mu(k)^2/k. Proof: n = ak, m = bk, mu(ak)mu(bk) = mu(a)mu(b)mu(k)^2 1_{(k,ab)=1}. So the correlation function of the Moebius vector on Q_+^x is explicit and product-form; normalised, it tends to the Fourier transform of the PRODUCT probability measure sigma = (x)_p (p/(p+1))|1 - p^(-1/2)chi_p|^2 dHaar, which is singular to Haar (Kakutani; from memory). Sum: pairs up to N. Family: nu, length T.
(b) NEAR SIDE (mollifier longer than the height; t-family T, N >> T). (*) holds for all N/T and reduces the near side EXACTLY to S_1(N) plus a logarithmically and coprimality-weighted sum of binary correlations mu(a)mu(a+h) at shifts h <= H = N/T. Off-diagonal is trivially O(H) against the diagonal (6/pi^2) log N: harmless up to N ~ T log T (Montgomery-Vaughan), beyond that it needs a POWER saving in H in averaged logarithmic Chowla. KNOWN (from memory, unread): Tao 2016 (fixed h, o(log x)), Matomaki-Radziwill-Tao 2015 (averaged, o(HX)): o(1) only. So (*) gives no new bound; it gives the exact location of the wall on the t-family.
