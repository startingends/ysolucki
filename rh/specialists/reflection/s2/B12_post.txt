(1) STRUCTURE, PROVED. Write both Grams as D [g(m/n)] D, D = diag(n^(-1/2)), q = m/n = a/b reduced: [1/max] has g_inf(q) = min(a/b,b/a)^(1/2), [1/lcm] has g_fin(q) = (ab)^(-1/2) = prod_p p^(-|v_p(q)|/2). They are the SAME local function min(|q|_v,|q|_v^(-1))^(1/2) at the archimedean place and at the finite places, and both are of Kallen-Lehmann type: g_inf = e^(-psi_inf/2), psi_inf = |sum_p v_p log p| (Poisson semigroup along the Kronecker line; Bochner weight 1/|s|^2), g_fin = e^(-psi_fin/2), psi_fin = sum_p |v_p| log p (product of lattice Poisson kernels of radius p^(-1/2)). So the two Moebius forms are the Poisson extensions of |M_N|^2 to ONE point, s = 1 = (p^(-1/2))_p in D^infinity, through two harmonic structures: the polydisc (profinite) and the half-plane pushed along the Bohr curve s -> (p^(1/2-s))_p (archimedean). Both are >= |sum mu(n)/n|^2, and they agree on pluriharmonic functions. The two generators differ by the TRIANGLE INEQUALITY: psi_inf <= psi_fin, defect 2 log min(a,b) (equality iff q or 1/q is an integer).
(2) THE NATURAL INTERPOLATION EXISTS AND IS NOT MONOTONE. Schoenberg (KNOWN, from memory): psi_theta = (1-theta) psi_inf + theta psi_fin is negative definite, so g_theta = g_inf^(1-theta) g_fin^theta is positive definite and generates a Markov convolution semigroup on T^infinity for every theta. (3) NO CONVOLUTION JOINS THE ENDPOINTS, in either direction (PROVED). nu_fin = nu_inf * kappa with kappa a probability measure (a random twist) would need kappa-hat = g_fin/g_inf = 1/min(a,b) positive definite on Q_+^x; on {1,2,3} its matrix is [[1,1,1],[1,1,1/2],[1,1/2,1]], determinant -1/4. The other ratio, min(a,b), is unbounded. So no Markov semigroup of convolution type, and no average over twists, carries one value to the other.
(4) WHICH HYPOTHESIS A MONOTONE SEMIGROUP MUST VIOLATE (HEURISTIC, with numbers). The comparison goes the wrong way for control: the archimedean value is the LARGER one (ratio 3.0-3.9 above), and under RH it should grow like c log N, c ~ sum_rho 1/(|zeta'(rho)|^2 |rho|^2) (each zero on the line resolved at scale 1/log N, the mechanism of my OBJECT_PHI.md Round 3), while the profinite value is a product of local Szego problems, each solved EXACTLY by the local Moebius factor 1 - p^(-1/2) z_p (|1 - r z|^2 P_r = 1 - r^2): it contains no zeros, so it stays bounded. A profinite value can therefore bound the archimedean one only from BELOW. A semigroup that made the profinite value control the archimedean one from above would have to create the zeros' singularities, i.e. couple the coordinates through sum_p v_p log p: it would have to be non-product and non-convolution, which is to say it would have to contain the archimedean place itself (barrier B10 again).
COROLLARY of (3): the 'interaction' function e^(-t log min(a,b)) is not positive definite at t = 1 (it is 1/min(a,b)), so the difference of the two structures is not itself a Markov semigroup there; for small t I have not checked (CONJECTURE: not positive definite for any t > 0, since min(a,b) is not conditionally negative definite).
