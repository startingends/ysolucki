## (a) The implication, with the verifier's correction built in
Lemma C (exact circularity of every copy amplifier; PROVED, one line). For every multiset F and every primitive chi:
    sum over the rows of F whose primitive character is chi of |A_row(D)|^2  <=  S(F, D).
The copy amplifier's output |A_1|^2 <= 2 S/C + 2 err^2 is this inequality for chi = 1, read backwards: the conclusion IS the copy-row part of
the hypothesis. The amplifier has content only if S is proved by a method that does not bound the copy rows one by one; with copy density
C/H ~ H^(-5/6) (October) the copy rows are a negligible part of S; with C/H = H^(-o(1)) (E3) they are a positive-proportion part of it.

Theorem A (PROVED). Fix eps > 0. Suppose that for every large D there is H <= D^(1+eps) with
  (H1') C*(H) := #{u <= H : psi_u trivial, every prime factor of u exceeds D^eps} >= H^(1-eps)     [rough copies]
  (H2') S(H, D) <= H D^(1+eps).
Then A_1(y) << y^(1/2 + 3 eps) for all y, hence (Mellin, two choices of W) zeta has no zero in Re s > 1/2 + 3eps.
Proof. Copy error: for a copy u, A_(psi_u)(D) - A_1(D) = - sum_{(n,u) > 1} mu(n) W(n/D) = sum over non-empty sets T of primes of u of
(-1)^|T| sum_{d_T | n} mu(n) W(n/D), d_T = prod_{p in T} p, and sum_{d_T | n} mu(n) W(n/D) = mu(d_T) sum_{(m, d_T) = 1} mu(m) W(d_T m/D).
Removing the coprimality one prime at a time (B(g) = sum_{j >= 0} A(g(p^j .))) writes it as a sum of at most (1/eps)^(1/eps) values A_1(y)
with y <= D/d_T <= D^(1-eps) (u <= H has at most (1 + eps)/eps prime factors, each > D^eps). Bootstrap: if |A_1(y)| <= K y^theta for all y,
then each copy has |A_u - A_1| <= K_eps K D^(theta(1-eps)), and the amplifier over the C* rough copies gives
  |A_1(D)|^2 <= 2 S/C* + 2 (K_eps K D^(theta(1-eps)))^2 <= 2 D^(1+3eps) + 2 K_eps^2 K^2 D^(2 theta (1-eps)).
So theta -> max(1/2 + 3eps/2, theta(1 - eps)); from theta = 1 finitely many steps (depending on eps only) reach 1/2 + 2eps. []

## (b) The converse, and the exact sense of 'circular'
(b1) PROVED (the standard consequence of GRH, recalled). GRH for every L(s, psi_u), u <= H (conductors <= (H m_0)^(O(1))) gives |A_(psi_u)(D)| << D^(1/2+eps) H^eps, hence (H2').
(b2) PROVED (Lemma C). (H2') at level D gives, for EVERY primitive chi in the family, |A_chi(D)|^2 << H D^(1+eps)/M_chi (plus copy-type
coprimality errors controlled by lower scales as in Theorem A): a GRH-strength bound for every character of multiplicity >= H^(1-eps).
So (H2') sits between 'GRH for the high-multiplicity characters at level D' (implied) and 'GRH for all members' (implying).
(b3) Theorem B (PROVED; it settles the OPEN conjecture of COPY_BARRIER E3 in the positive-density, bounded-order case).
Call the family POISSON-ABLE if for every n the map u -> psi_u(n) is a Dirichlet character chi_n of u with conductor dividing (n m_0)^inf
(the October first step). Assume all psi_u have order dividing a fixed m, and P_0 has positive upper density. Then the family is DEGENERATE:
there is a finite set E of primes such that, on n coprime to E u S, psi_u(n) depends only on u mod M (M = m_0^b, b depending on m, S);
so F_H contains at most phi(M) m^|E| distinct primitive characters, each with multiplicity >> H.
Proof. (1) Bounded order: every psi_p, p in P_0, is unramified outside S, so its conductor divides M. Hence for q, q' outside S with
q = q' mod M: chi_q(p) = psi_p(q) = psi_p(q') = chi_q'(p) for all p in P_0 (except q, q').
(2) Let Q_ram = {q not in S : chi_q ramified at q}. If Q_ram were infinite, pigeonhole gives q_1, q_2, ... in Q_ram in one class mod M.
xi_j := chi_(q_1) conj(chi_(q_j)) has a non-trivial q_j-component and trivial q_i-component for i != j, i >= 2 (conductor of chi_n divides
(n m_0)^inf), so <xi_2, ..., xi_k> has order >= 2^(k-1); by (1) it is trivial on P_0 minus a finite set; by Dirichlet the primes in the
kernel of a group of characters of order N have density 1/N. So P_0 has upper density <= 2^(1-k) for every k: contradiction.
(3) So E := Q_ram is finite and, for q outside S u E, chi_q is unramified at q: conductor dividing m_0^inf, order dividing m, so dividing M.
For n coprime to S u E: psi_u(n) = prod_q chi_q(u)^(v_q(n)) depends on u mod M only. []
CONSEQUENCE (exact circularity, the lead's S2 B9 sense). For a Poisson-able family with P_0 of positive density, (H2') at level D is
EQUIVALENT (two-sided, up to D^eps and the lower-scale coprimality errors) to |A_chi(D)| << D^(1/2+eps) for the finitely many fixed
characters chi of conductor dividing M that occur, the trivial one included: at each finite level it is the conclusion (RH and GRH for
finitely many fixed characters) at that same level. Nothing a limit removes, no margin.
