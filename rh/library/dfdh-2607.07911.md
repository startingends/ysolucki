## 2. Main statements
Xi_n(A,B) := sup over beta != 0 of ||beta||^{-2} sum_{N(a) <= A, a = 1} mu^2(a) |sum_{N(b) <= B} mu^2(b) beta_b (a/b)_n|^2
(n = 3 over Z[zeta_3], a, b = 1 mod 3; n = 4 over Z[i], = 1 mod lambda^3).
- Known upper bound (Blomer-Goldmakher-Louvel Thm 1.3; Heath-Brown 2000 for n = 3):
  Xi_n(A,B) << (AB)^eps (A + B + (AB)^{2/3}).
- **Theorem 1.1 (unconditional).** Xi_3(A,B) >> (AB)^{-eps} (A + B + (AB)^{2/3}): the cubic large sieve is optimal.
  (Makes Dunn-Radziwill Thm 1.4 unconditional.)
- **Theorem 1.2 (unconditional).** Xi_4(A,B) >> (AB)^{-eps} (A + B + A^{3/4}B^{1/2} + A^{1/2}B^{3/4}).
- **Conjecture 1.3.** Xi_4(A,B) << (AB)^eps (A + B + A^{3/4}B^{1/2} + A^{1/2}B^{3/4}).
- **Conjecture (1.6), general n >= 3, K containing the n-th roots of unity.**
  `Xi_n(A,B) = (AB)^{o(1)} (A + B + A^{1-1/n} B^{2/n} + A^{2/n} B^{1-1/n})`.
  "Any power-saving improvement on the (AB)^{2/3+eps} term ... for any n >= 4 would be a substantial advance."
- **Proof of Theorem 1.1** (section 2): duality Xi_3(A,B) ~ Xi_3(B,A); for B <= A < B^2 take
  beta_b = conj(g~_3(b)) H(N(b)/B); the b-sum equals a polar term P_B(a) ~ C_1 conj(g~_3(a)) N(a)^{-1/6} B^{5/6}
  (the theta pole, via DDDS24 Lemma 6.7) plus a remainder bounded through psi^{(3)}(a/d, 1 + eps + iy); the remainder's
  mean square over a is << (AB)^{1+eps} by the Lindelöf-on-average bound (2.6)
  `sum_{N(a) <= A} |psi^{(3)}(a, 1 + eps + it)|^2 << A^{1+eps} (1+|t|)^R` (DDDS24 Lemma 6.6, itself proved with the
  cubic large sieve), while the polar terms give >> A^{2/3-eps} B^{5/3}. Hence Xi_3 >> (AB)^{2/3-eps}.

## 3. Method in ten lines (cubic part)
The bias of cubic Gauss sums (theta pole at s = 4/3 of psi(r,s) = sum_c g_3(r,c) N(c)^{-s}, residue proportional to
the theta coefficient tau_3(r)) makes the Gauss-sum-weighted sequence correlate with EVERY cubic character (a/.)_3
at size B^{5/6} N(a)^{-1/6}; summed over a this beats A + B. The only analytic inputs are Patterson's exact theta
coefficients and a Lindelöf-on-average bound in the twist aspect at Re s = 1 + eps. For n = 4, where the quartic
theta coefficients are unknown, both inputs are replaced by Rankin-Selberg integrals of quartic Eisenstein and
theta series with a Zagier-type regularization (not read).

## 4. Where it stops
Cubic: optimal up to (AB)^eps. Quartic: lower bound only; the upper bound is Conjecture 1.3. General n: conjecture
(1.6) only. Level structure with uniformity (sums of tau_n(r_1 a) conj(tau_n(r_2 a))) is avoided by special tricks
for n = 4.

## 5. What it changes in our record
- **SHORT_FAMILY.md (2d)** quotes the sextic large sieve of the QRH 7/8 paper (Lemma 9.1):
  (KD)^eps (K + D + (KD)^{2/3}). This paper shows the analogous term is NOT removable for n = 3 (theorem) and
  conjectures, for n = 6 over Q(zeta_6) = Q(omega), the norm (KD)^{o(1)}(K + D + K^{5/6}D^{1/3} + K^{1/3}D^{5/6})
  (reader's substitution n = 6 in (1.6)). At K = D this conjectured norm is D^{7/6}, so even the conjectured sextic
  sieve exceeds K + D near the self-dual point; the excess is carried by Gauss-sum-weighted sequences. Whether the
  Möbius coefficients mu(n) nu(n) of S(H,D) see this excess is not addressed in any text read.
- **SHORT_FAMILY (3d), "Lindelöf on average over h" for twisted theta series.** A Lindelöf-on-average bound in the
  twist aspect for the Dirichlet series of cubic Gauss sums is PROVED (DDDS24 Lemma 6.6, quoted here as (2.6); see
  also the statement in arXiv 2410.03048 section 6: sum_{N(m) <= M} |psi(hm, sigma + it)|^2 << N(h)^{1/2} M^{1+eps}
  (1+|t|)^2 for 1 < sigma <= 5/4). It is at Re s > 1 (just right of the centre s = 1 of the functional equation
  s <-> 2 - s), with family size M equal to the conductor size; READER-7 did not check how this compares with the
  statement needed in SHORT_FAMILY (3d) (twists of conductor up to D^{3/2}, sums of length D).
