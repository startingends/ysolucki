## 2. What it states (parts read)
- A(λ) := ∫_0^∞ {t}{λt} dt/t² (λ ≥ 0), the autocorrelation of the fractional part in 𝓗; A(λ) = λA(1/λ); A > 0;
  Mellin transform MA(s) = −ζ(−s)ζ(s+1)/(s(s+1)) on −1 < Re s < 0 (Prop. t122); A(1) = log 2π − γ (Prop. t123).
- **Prop. t125.** For coprime p, q: A(p/q) = ((1−λ)/2) log λ + ((λ+1)/2)(log 2π − γ) − (π/(2q))(V(p,q) + V(q,p)),
  λ = p/q, V = Vasyunin's cotangent-type sums. (This is the closed form of the Nyman–Beurling Gram entries, since
  ⟨e_k, e_l⟩ is a rescaled A(k/l); lane B of the project used "Vasyunin formulas" for the Gram matrix, IDEAS #5.)
- Prop. t124, t106, t129: relations between A and φ_1(λ) = Σ_k B_1(kλ)/k (B_1 the first periodic Bernoulli function).
- **Prop. t128 (local behaviour at rationals).** For 0 < |t| ≤ 1/(2q):
  A(p/q + t) − A(p/q) = |t| log|t|/(2p) + D^±(p,q) t − q(p+q±1)t²/(4p²) + O(q⁴p^{−1}t³), with an explicit D^±(p,q)
  involving V(q,p). So A is continuous, nowhere-smooth at rationals (a |t| log|t| cusp at every rational).
- A graph of A(λ) on the Farey sequence of order 287 is included (figure not opened).
- **BDBLS 2000, as quoted by others** (secondary): unconditionally liminf D(λ)√(log 1/λ) ≥ √(Σ_ρ 1/|ρ|²) (zeros counted
  once; Burnol's Theorem 1.2), in the discrete form liminf d_N² log N ≥ Σ_{Re ρ = ½} 1/|ρ|² (BCF); and the conjecture
  d_N² ~ (2 + γ − log 4π)/log N (Balazard–de Roton: "les auteurs de [BDBLS2000] conjecturent l'égalité"), supported by
  numerics (Burnol §5).

## 3. Method
Elementary real analysis of {t}: Mellin–Plancherel, Euler–Maclaurin-type summation, and reciprocity for Vasyunin
sums. No zeros of ζ enter.

## 4-5. Assumptions and where it stops
Unconditional. It computes the Gram kernel exactly; it says nothing about the size of d_N.
