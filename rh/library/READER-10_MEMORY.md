# READER-10 memory (reader seat): answer to the brief's knowledge question (knowledge only, no direction)
Q: does any published construction give, at ∞ of ℚ, a cusp WITHOUT a continuous direction, or a window/cutoff at ∞ with a bound fixed under the amplifier? **Answer: NO** in all three texts read.
1. Haran, arXiv 1508.04636 (`haran-1508.04636.md`): NO. η is a genuine valuation/point of a compact \bar{Spec ℤ} (§2.5 Ostrowski I; §5.2), but Ch. 6, Ex. 6.3.1, (6.3.9)/(6.3.20): rank-d bundles = GL_d(ℚ)\GL_d(𝔸)/(O_d×∏GL_d(ℤ_p)), the usual adelic quotient with its archimedean height; (6.3.23) gives Pic_1 = ℚ*\𝔸*/∏O* ≅ ℝ_{>0}. Ch. 6 summary: the formalism "creat[es] the continuum ℝ^+". No reduction theory, no bound (keyword scan of unread chapters: nothing).
2. Deninger, arXiv 0709.2801 (`deninger-0709.2801.md`): NO. ∞ ↔ FIXED POINTS of the flow on a compactification (Dictionary part 2; remark after Construction 3). The only positivity (Re ρ = α/2, Theorem 5, §3) is for compact X without fixed points, i.e. no infinite place, and then "the conditions ... force α = 0"; the archimedean term (1−e^{−2t})^{−1} is explicitly missing (§3), and the fixed-point case is "optimism" (§3, §5).
3. Harder (original Ann. Math. 1974 NOT reachable; `harder-compact-support.md`): the function-field bound is confirmed: Tian Thm 2.7, every cuspidal φ of level K is supported in one fixed C_K, finite mod Z(𝔸)G(F),K; Xue (1802.01362, Introduction; Prop. `prop-TC-isom-mu-grand`): on deep HN strata constant term = value. The mechanism needs a discrete cusp (HN degrees in a lattice cone) and an open K_v containing unipotents (Buzzard §7.2). No text treats number fields; the failure over ℚ (K_∞ ∩ N(ℝ) = {1}, continuous y, decay instead of vanishing) is from memory.
Not read: Haran's 1993 "Index theory, potential theory and RH" and 2001 "Mysteries of the real prime" (not on arXiv); Connes-Moscovici prolate (already in INDEX: 2112.05500).
