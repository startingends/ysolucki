## 2. Main statements
Proved, unconditional, with written proofs in the text:
- **Theorem 1.1** (one-stroke reduction). For `T#` with `beta* := log(T#/2 pi) - 1/T# - A_L > 0` (this forces
  `T# > T_1`): for every real even `f` supported in `[-L, L]`,
  "Q(f) >= R(f) := 2F(i/2)^2 + (1/pi) int_0^{T#} (Psi_L(t) - beta*) |F(t)|^2 dt + beta* ||f||_2^2."
  In the Legendre basis `R = beta* I + 2pp^T + C` with `C` super-exponentially small beyond orders about
  `e L T#/2`; if the leading `N x N` block has least eigenvalue `>= lambda_0 > 0`, then
  "Q(f) >= (min(lambda_0, beta* - eps_D) - eps_B) ||f||_2^2 for all f with supp f in [-L, L]", with explicit
  tail constants `eps_D`, `eps_B`. (Proof: `Psi_L(t) >= log(t/2 pi) - 1/t - A_L` for `t >= 15/4`, Lemma 3.1;
  `|j_n(x)| <= x^n/(2n+1)!!`; a two-block eigenvalue bound.)
- **Lemma 3.2.** `sup_t sum_{log n < 2L} 2 Lambda(n) n^{-1/2} cos(t log n) = A_L` (attained at 0 and approached at
  arbitrarily large `t`, by linear independence of the `log p` and Weyl equidistribution). **Remark 3.3**: the
  infimum is `-A_eff` with `A_eff < A_L` (e.g. 4.6948 against 7.0750 at `L = 1.19`), which bounds the symbol from
  the wrong side for a certificate. Table of thresholds: `(L, A_L, T_1)` = (0.8, 2.9420, 119), (1.0, 5.8525,
  2187), (1.19, 7.0750, 7.4e3), (1.2, 8.5210, 3.2e4), (1.4, 10.290, 1.9e5), (1.6, 14.323, 1.0e7),
  (2.0, 24.383, 2.4e11).
- **Theorem 1.4** (barrier). "Any application of Theorem 1.1 requires T# > T_1(L) = 2 pi e^{A_L}, and
  A_L = (4 + o(1)) e^L as L -> inf by the prime number theorem; hence the matrix size N ~ L T_1 and the number
  of quadrature nodes grow doubly exponentially in L. Moreover sup_t of the prime comb equals A_L exactly, so
  within the class of certificates that bound the comb pointwise the threshold T_1 cannot be lowered."
- **Lemma 2.5** (exact time-domain archimedean term): for `g` even, continuous, supported in `[-2L, 2L]`,
  `(1/2 pi) int ghat(t)[Re psi(1/4 + it/2) - log pi] dt = -(gamma + log pi + log(1 - e^{-4L})) g(0)
  + int_0^{2L} 2[e^{-2x} g(0) - e^{-x/2} g(x)]/(1 - e^{-2x}) dx`.
- **Lemma 6.1** (parity). `Q(f) = Q(f_e) + Q(f_o)`; the pole term is `+2(int f_e cosh(x/2))^2` on the even part
  and `-2(int f_o sinh(x/2))^2` on the odd part; for complex `f`, `Q(f) = Q(Re f) + Q(Im f)`.

Proved conditionally on RH:
- **Proposition 2.3.** "Assume RH. For every L > 0 there is c_L > 0 such that Q(f) >= c_L ||f||_2^2 for all

## 3. Method in ten lines
1. On a window the Weil form is a rank-one pole term plus a Fourier multiplier `Psi_L` with finitely many
   primes.
2. Lower bounds: for large `t` the digamma term grows like `log(t/2 pi)` while the prime comb is at most `A_L`;
   so beyond `T# > 2 pi e^{A_L}` the symbol is `>= beta* > 0` pointwise.
3. Replace the symbol by `beta*` beyond `T#`: this only lowers the form, and what remains is `beta*` times the
   identity plus a multiplier supported on `[0, T#]` plus the pole.
4. Functions supported in `[-L, L]` expanded in Legendre polynomials have transforms (spherical Bessel
   functions) that are super-exponentially small on `[0, T#]` beyond order about `e L T#/2`: the infinite matrix
   is a finite block plus a tail equal to `beta* I` up to `1e-100`.
5. Certify the finite block: Gauss quadrature with Bernstein-ellipse error bounds, then a Cholesky
   factorisation of the shifted matrix with a verified residual.
6. Parity: the same reduction in the odd sector; an upper bound for the even floor and a Courant-Fischer lower
   bound for the second even value give "simple and even".
7. Upper bounds: any trial function gives one. Propose it from the zeros side (sum of non-negative terms, first
   1000 zeros in multiprecision, tail model), then evaluate its Rayleigh quotient on the geometric side in
   interval arithmetic deep enough to resolve up to 280 digits of cancellation.
8. Read the decay off the table; compare two candidate laws out of sample.
9. Conditional theorem: kill the zeros up to `T = 2 pi e^L` with a canonical product and pay for its growth
   with a power-of-sinc window of type `L`; the potential-theoretic cost is computed in closed form.
10. Barrier: the threshold in step 2 is `2 pi e^{A_L}`, `A_L ~ 4 e^L`, and by Lemma 3.2 no pointwise bound on
    the comb lowers it.

## 5. Where its frontier is
**Last things proved or certified.** (i) Unconditional positivity of the window form with an explicit constant
on `[-0.8, 0.8]` (primes 2, 3 and the prime power 4 in the window), all complex test functions; simple even
ground state there. (ii) Unconditional upper bounds for the even floor out to `L = 2`. (iii) Under RH,
`lambda*(L) <= exp(-L e^L)` for large `L`.

**First things it cannot do.**
- Certify positivity at any `L > 0.8`. At `L = 1.19` the paper retracts its earlier claim: "An earlier draft of
  this work claimed a certified theorem at that support, on the basis of a per-prime 'sharpening' of the
  envelope constant which in fact bounds the prime comb in the wrong direction ... We retract the claim." The
  corrected cost: `T# ~ 8.5e3` to `1.2e4`, `N ~ 1.4e4` to `2e4` Legendre orders, about 90 digits; "We have not
  carried it out." At `L = 1` the threshold is `T_1 = 2187`. "Certifying positivity past support about 3.2
  (T_1 ~ 1e7) by any pointwise-envelope method is therefore computationally void."
- Prove the decay law. The scale `T*` and the constant `2 pi^2` "remain conjectural"; the theorem under RH
  reaches only the exponent `e^L` ("the construction operates at half saturation"); Remark 13.6: the same
  construction "closes only for a <~ 0.65" of the way to `e^{2L}`.
- Prove any unconditional LOWER bound on `lambda*(L)` as a function of `L`: "a lower bound of the form
  lambda*(L) >= e^{-C N(T*)} valid unconditionally for all L would imply RH".
- Prove that the cost of an arbitrary positivity certificate (not only pointwise-envelope ones) must grow:
  "converting it into a proved lower bound on the cost of an arbitrary positivity certificate is open".
- Lower the threshold `T_1`: this "is a statement about linear forms in {log p}", "an arithmetic problem,
  concerning simultaneous Diophantine properties of {log p}, and not a computational one."

## 6. What it explicitly does not do (checked over the whole text)
- No statement on zeros of the transform of the ground state (their reality or location); the figure of the
  minimiser at `L = 1` is described as showing `|fhat|^2` suppressed "at every zero below T* ~ 46".
- No variation of the arithmetic data: the weights `Lambda(n)` and positions `log n` are never perturbed; no
  function other than zeta; no control without Euler product. (Dirichlet L-functions are an open problem (iv).)
- No continuity, monotonicity or derivative statement for `lambda*(L)`.
- `lambda*` is the EVEN-sector infimum by definition; equality with the full bottom of the spectrum is
  certified at `L = 0.8`, observed for `L <= 1.2`, "spot-checked" at `L = 1.4`, and not addressed beyond.
- The "second missing step" of Connes-Consani-Moscovici, "convergence of the approximate ground-state zeros to
  the zeta zeros as the scale grows, is untouched".
- Section 15 lists four abandoned routes: Galerkin solve-lemmas; "symbol capping" below the threshold
  ("provably fatal ... the form becomes indefinite (measured lambda_min ~ -2.7). Capping the symbol amounts to
  admitting fake zeros"); block certificates in Legendre-order space ("the prime terms are shift operators, so
  any hard cut ... leaves O(1) coupling"); per-prime envelope sharpening (the retracted step).
- Section 10 documents three float64 pipelines that produced spurious negative eigenvalues or spurious
  exponential laws (rates 2.64 and 1.52), "a trap for any numerical study of Weil positivity".
- Internal loose ends noticed by this reader (they do not affect the statements above): the working precision
  at `L = 2.0` is given as 560 digits in Table 1, "330-digit" in section 11.5 and "365-digit" in section 17;
  Remark 11.6 says the `L = 2.0` value "settled ... at 4.2e-283" while Table 1 has `3.19e-283` (the text says
  geometric values are "typically a few percent sharper" than zeros-side ones).
