## 2. Main theorems (all unconditional; "none of them depends on RH")
Objects. The screw function (1.3):
`g(t) = -4(e^{t/2}+e^{-t/2}-2) + sum_{n <= e^{|t|}} Lambda(n) n^{-1/2} (|t| - log n) - (|t|/2)(psi(1/4) - log pi)
 - (1/4)(Phi(1,2,1/4) - e^{-|t|/2} Phi(e^{-2|t|},2,1/4))`, continuous, real, even; formally the distribution
`k(t) = sum_gamma e^{-i gamma t}` integrated twice. `G u = g * u`; `L_0^2(-a,a)` = mean-zero functions;
`G_a = P_a G P_a` on `L_0^2(-a,a)`; `D = i d/dx` with Dirichlet conditions; `B_a = D^* G_a D` with domain
`H_0^1(-a,a)` (symmetric, not self-adjoint). `A_a` is the self-adjoint operator with
`Q_W^a(v) = <A_a v, v>` (existence quoted from Connes-Consani-Moscovici).

- **Theorem 1.1.** "For each a>0, the self-adjoint operator A_a defined by (1.1) is the Friedrichs extension of
  the symmetric operator B_a defined by (1.6)."
- **Corollary 1.2.** "For each a>0, any function attaining the infimum in (1.7) belongs to the closure of
  C_c^infty(-a,a) with respect to the form norm associated with Q_W^a. In particular, lambda_a is the infimum
  of the Rayleigh quotient <B_a v, v>/||v||^2 over C_c^infty(-a,a)."
- **Theorem 1.3.** "The lowest eigenvalue lambda_a is continuous in a."
- **Theorem 1.4.** "For sufficiently small a>0, the lowest eigenvalue lambda_a is positive, simple, and satisfies
  lambda_a = log(1/a) + mu_1 - log(2 pi) + psi(2) - 1 + O(a) as a -> 0+, for some constant mu_1 > 0. Furthermore,
  the corresponding eigenfunction is even." (`mu_1` is the lowest eigenvalue of the operator `T` of the form
  (4.4) on `(-1,1)`; its value is not computed. "Sufficiently small" is not quantified.)
- **Theorem 1.5.** Fix `lambda < lambda_a`, `T_a = A_a - lambda`, `H(T_a)` = completion of `C_c^infty(-a,a)` for
  `<T_a v, v>`, `D_a = i d/dx` on `H(T_a)` with domain `C_c^infty(-a,a)` (symmetric, deficiency indices (1,1):
  Lemmas 6.1, 6.2), self-adjoint extensions `D_{a,theta}`. With `v_±` eigenfunctions of `D_a^*` for `±i`,
  normalised to equal `T_a`-norm, the function
  `W(a,theta;z) := (z-i) int_{-a}^a v_+(a,x) e^{izx} dx + e^{i theta}(z+i) int_{-a}^a v_-(a,x) e^{izx} dx`
  "is entire in z. The eigenvalues of the self-adjoint operator D_{a,theta} are precisely the zeros of
  W(a,theta;z). Furthermore, all zeros of W(a,theta;z) are real." Every eigenvalue has multiplicity one
  (section 6.3). Holds for every `a`, every `theta`, every `lambda < lambda_a`, with no simplicity or parity
  hypothesis and whether or not the form is positive.
- **Corollary 1.6** (conditional statement; the paper calls it its "main conjectural statement"). "If one can
  choose lambda = lambda(a) < lambda_a, theta = theta(a), and phi(a,z) for all sufficiently large a>0 such that
  phi(a,z) is analytic in the upper half-plane and lim_{a->inf} e^{phi(a,z)} W(a,theta;z) =
  xi(1/2-iz)/(xi(1/2-iz)+xi'(1/2-iz)) holds uniformly on every compact subset of the upper half-plane, then RH
  holds."
- Supporting statements: **Lemma 3.1** `B_a subset A_a`. **Proposition 4.1** `H^log(I) -> L^2(I)` is compact for
  a bounded interval `I` (`H^log` = `{int (1+log^+|z|)|vhat|^2 < inf}`; proof referred to CCM25 Thm 3.6).
  (4.10): `lambda_a = min(lambda_a^+, lambda_a^-)`, the infima over even and odd functions.
- Formulas proved on the way (section 2), for `v in H_0^1(-a,a)`:
  - (2.2) near 0: `g(t) = (1/2)|t| log|t| + A|t| + sum_{n<=e^{|t|}} Lambda(n) n^{-1/2}(|t|-log n) + r(t)`,
    `r` even, `C^2`, `O(t^2)`, `A = (log 2pi - psi(2))/2 = 0.707546...`.
  - (2.3),(2.5): `Q_W^a(v) = L_a(v) - (2A+1)||v||^2 - sum_{n<=e^{2a}} Lambda(n) n^{-1/2} (int v(x+log n) conj v(x)
    + int v(x-log n) conj v(x)) - iint r''(x-y) v(y) conj v(x)`, with
    `L_a(v) = (1/4) iint_{(-a,a)^2} |v(x)-v(y)|^2/|x-y| dx dy - (1/2) int log(a^2-x^2)|v(x)|^2 dx`.
  - (2.6): `L_a(v) = (1/2pi) int (log|z| + C_0)|vhat(z)|^2 dz`.
  - (2.8): `Q_W^a(v) = (1/2pi) int (log|z| - log 2pi)|vhat|^2 dz + O(a)||v||^2` (`O(a)`: a bounded quantity
    depending on `a`), agreeing with Bombieri's Lemma 3.
  - (2.10),(2.11): on `C_c^infty(-a,a)`, `A_a v = (-g'') * v` with
    `-g'' = -(1/2)Pf(1/|t|) - (2A+1) delta - sum_n Lambda(n) n^{-1/2}(delta(t-log n)+delta(t+log n)) - r''`
    ("an alternative representation of Bombieri's Lagrangian").
  - (4.5), on the fixed interval, `w(t) = v(at)`: `R(a,w) = -log a - (2A+1) + L(w)/||w||^2 - (prime terms with
    shifts (log n)/a, n <= e^{2a}) - (a/||w||^2) iint r''(a(x-y)) w(y) conj w(x)`.
  - (8.2),(8.4): for `u = Dv`: `||v||^2 = <K_a u,u>`, `K_a = (-Delta_N)^{-1}` (inverse Neumann Laplacian, kernel
    `N(x,y) = (x^2+y^2)/(4a) - |x-y|/2 + a/6`), so `Q_W^a(v)/||v||^2 = <G_a u,u>/<K_a u,u>` and the spectrum of
    `A_a` is that of the generalised eigenvalue problem `G_a u = lambda K_a u` (two compact operators with
    continuous kernels). Bombieri's "Problem 1" (Rayleigh quotient with the `H^1` norm) is the ordinary
    eigenvalue problem of the compact operator `G_a` (section 8.4).

## 3. Method in ten lines
1. Integrate the zero-side distribution twice: the Weil form becomes `Q_W(v) = <G Dv, Dv>` with a continuous
   kernel `g` (Suzuki 2023, Prop. 3.1), so `B_a = D^* G_a D` is an honest symmetric operator on `H_0^1`.
2. Expand `g` at 0 (Lerch function, Gradshteyn-Ryzhik 9.554): leading term `(1/2)|t| log|t|`. Under `D^* . D`
   this gives the form `L_a`, with Fourier symbol `log|z| + C_0`; the rest is bounded.
3. Thm 1.1: `B_a subset A_a` by the zero-side formula tested on the exponentials `e_n = exp(i pi n x/a)`, which
   are a form core (Connes-Consani Prop. 2.3); then each `e_n` is approximated in form norm by cut-offs in
   `C_c^infty(-a,a)`, with error `<< eps log(1/eps)`. So the closure of the form of `B_a` is the whole form.
4. Thm 1.3: rescale to `(-1,1)`; the form splits as an `a`-independent closed form (`L - (2A+1)`) plus terms
   continuous in `(a,w)` (finitely many translations, continuous kernels).
5. Upper semicontinuity: test the minimiser of `a_0` (approximated in `C_c^infty`) at nearby `a`.
6. Lower semicontinuity: minimisers along `a_n -> a_0` are bounded in `H^log(-1,1)`; the compact embedding
   gives an `L^2` limit; closed forms are lower semicontinuous.
7. Thm 1.4: for `a < (1/2) log 2` no prime term is present and `R(a,v) = log(1/a) - (2A+1) + L(v)/||v||^2 + O(a)`.
8. `L` is an irreducible Dirichlet form (jump kernel `1/|x-y|`, killing `-log(1-x^2) >= 0`), so its semigroup
   is positivity improving (Lenz-Stollmann-Veselic): positive, simple ground state. Kato's perturbation
   theorems carry simplicity to `lambda_a` for small `a`; a simple ground state of fixed sign is even.
9. Thm 1.5: in `H(T_a)`, `D_a^* v = z v` iff `T_a v = C e^{-izx}`; `T_a` is invertible, so every `z` is a simple
   eigenvalue of `D_a^*` with eigenvector `v_z` = Riesz representer of `v -> vhat(conj z)`. Von Neumann's boundary
   form applied to `v_z` and `v_+ + e^{i theta} v_-` is `W(a,theta;z)`; its zeros are eigenvalues of a
   self-adjoint operator, hence real.
10. The work is in steps 2, 3 and 6. Step 9 uses nothing arithmetic: "its proof does not require detailed
    information on the arithmetic terms ... It relies essentially only on the fact that the prime contribution
    to Q_W^a involves only finitely many primes for fixed a>0."

## 4. What is assumed
- Theorems 1.1 to 1.5: no unproved hypothesis. They rest on results quoted, not reproved: lower bound and lower
  semicontinuity of `Q_W^a` and the core of exponentials (Connes-Consani, "Spectral triples and zeta-cycles",
  Prop. 2.1 and 2.3); existence of `A_a`, discreteness of its spectrum, existence of a ground state
  (Connes-Consani-Moscovici, "Zeta spectral triples", arXiv 2511.22755, section 3, Thm 3.6, Cor. 3.7, an
  unrefereed preprint); `Q_W(v) = <G Dv, Dv>` and "g is a screw function iff RH" (Suzuki 2023); the explicit
  formula in Bombieri's form (Bombieri 2001, Lemma 3). Proposition 4.1 is given without a written proof
  ("essentially the same as" CCM25 Thm 3.6).
- The new derivation of Yoshida's theorem (after Thm 1.3) also uses Yoshida's Lemma 2 (positivity for small
  `a`), which Theorem 1.4 reproves.
- Section 7: "Throughout this section, we assume RH", and "Some of the arguments below are heuristic and do not
  constitute rigorous proofs."
- Corollary 1.6 assumes the limit formula (1.12).

## 5. Where its frontier is
**Last things proved.** (i) For every window the form is the Friedrichs form of an explicit operator, and the
bottom eigenvalue `lambda_a` is a continuous function of `a` (Thm 1.3). (ii) For small `a` only:
`lambda_a > 0`, simple, even ground state, with the expansion of Thm 1.4. (iii) For every window, an entire
function `W(a,theta;z)` with only real zeros, each an eigenvalue of multiplicity one of a self-adjoint
operator (Thm 1.5).

**First things it cannot do, in the paper's own words or by what its proofs use.**
- Positivity, simplicity and evenness beyond "sufficiently small a": the proof of Thm 1.4 is a perturbation of
  the prime-free regime `a < (1/2) log 2` ("as a -> 0+"); no value of `a` is given and no window containing a
  prime is treated. Nothing is proved about the sign or simplicity of `lambda_a` for larger `a`.
- The ground state `v_a` itself: the reality of the zeros of `vhat_a` is NOT proved here. The paper says of
  Connes-Consani-Moscovici (CCM25 Thm 5.10): "what is actually established there is not directly the reality of
  the zeros of vhat_a(z) itself, but rather the reality of the zeros of the Fourier transform of a function
  minimizing the Rayleigh quotient of Q_W^a|_V" for finite-dimensional `V`, and under the hypotheses "simplicity
  of lambda_a and the evenness of the corresponding eigenfunction" (i.e. `dim E^+(lambda_a) = 1`,
  `E^-(lambda_a) = 0`). Suzuki's `W(a,theta;z)` is a different function (built from `v_{±i}`, not from `v_a`).
- The limit `a -> infinity`. The CCM limit `lim c_a vhat_a(z) = xi(1/2+iz)` (1.2) is called "conjectural". The
  paper's own limit (1.12) is unproved, and the author writes: "we expect that proving (1.12) will require
  lambda = 0 for all sufficiently large a>0, which is equivalent to RH by Weil's criterion. In this sense,
  Corollary 1.6 does not appear to provide a simple route to a proof of RH."
- Section 7 (assuming RH) explains why (1.12) is expected: `H(A_inf)` (completion of `C_c^infty(R)` for `Q_W`)
  is isometric to a de Branges space generated by `E(z) = xi(1/2-iz) + xi'(1/2-iz)` (Suzuki 2025); the generator
  of translations on it is a self-adjoint operator with spectrum `Gamma` ("a Hilbert-Polya operator is realized
  as the infinitesimal generator of the translation group under the assumption A_inf > 0 (RH)"); the boundary
  form at `theta = pi` gives `(2i/pi) xi'(3/2) xi(1/2-iz)`. For the window spaces: "the convergence of these
  spaces is understood only heuristically, and no precise notion of convergence is specified." The reproducing
  kernel `J_a(z,z) = sup |vhat(z)|^2 / Q_W^a(v)` "may become unbounded on the diagonal in the limit"; "we do
  not pursue this issue further in the present paper, and leave it for future study."
- Numerics: one sentence only. "Our numerical experiments provide evidence supporting (1.12) for the choice
  theta = pi." No data, no range of `a`, no method beyond "solving this Fredholm equation numerically".

## 6. What it explicitly does not do (checked over the whole text)
- No monotonicity statement for `lambda_a`, no derivative of `lambda_a` in `a`, no rate of decay of `lambda_a`
  for large `a`, no value of `lambda_a` at any `a`.
- No analysis of a degenerate (null) vector: the first-failure statement appears only as existence, in one
  paragraph after Thm 1.3 ("if RH is false, then Q_W^a must be degenerate for some value of a by continuity").
- No continuity statement for the parity-resolved bottoms `lambda_a^+`, `lambda_a^-` separately. Theorem 1.3 is
  for their minimum. Section 4.4 notes that continuity of `lambda_a^±` "is asserted in [Bombieri 2001, Theorem
  5], although the details of the proof are not fully provided there".
- No example other than zeta: no L-function, no function without Euler product, no control case.
- No use of the Euler product beyond "finitely many prime powers below e^{2a}", and no use of the size of
  `Lambda(n)`: the prime terms enter only as a bounded perturbation continuous in `(a,w)`.
- No identification of the form domain: only `D(Q_W^a) subset H^log(-a,a)` is proved. The paper remarks that
  Bombieri (2003, Thm 5.1) "appears to state implicitly" that the domain is
  `{iint |v(x)-v(y)|^2/|x-y| + int |v|^2 < inf}` and that "this space is in fact slightly larger".
- The zero-side formula (3.1) is quoted without a stated class of functions; it is used for `v in H_0^1(-a,a)`
  against the exponentials `e_n`, where both transforms are `O(1/|z|)`.

## 8. Open questions the paper itself states
- The limit formula (1.12) of Corollary 1.6 (its "main conjectural statement"), including "the precise form of
  the limiting formula", which depends on how the singularity of `J_a` on the diagonal, "and any possible
  singularity off the diagonal", behave as `a -> infinity`: "It is not clear whether J_a(z,w) also develops a
  singularity off the diagonal in this limit."
- Whether `D_{a,theta(a)} -> D` (the translation generator on `H(A_inf)`) "in a suitable sense after removing the
  contribution from N" (expected, not formulated).
- "It is natural to ask whether D_psi can be characterized directly as a self-adjoint extension of [the closure
