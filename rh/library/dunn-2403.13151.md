## 2. Main statements, with their numbers
Setting: Gamma = SL_2(Z[omega]), Gamma_2 = <SL_2(Z), Gamma_1(3)>, cubic Kubota character chi on Gamma_2; f a cuspidal
cubic metaplectic Maass form on Gamma_2 (Delta f = -tau_f(2 - tau_f) f), Fourier coefficients rho_f(nu),
nu in lambda^{-3} Z[omega]. Sums: P_f(X,v,u;W_K) = sum_{lambda^3 nu = u mod v} rho_f(nu) Lambda(lambda^3 nu) W_K(N(nu)/X).
- **Theorem 1.1.** P_f(X,v,u;W_K) << (X K N(v))^eps K^8 N(v)^4 X^{1 - 1/34}.
- **Corollary 1.2.** Sharp-cutoff versions: P_f(X,v,u), and the sum over primes varpi = u mod v of
  rho_f(lambda^{-3} varpi) log N(varpi), are << X^{1 - 1/578 + eps} (the cubic analogue of Duke-Iwaniec 1990 for
  half-integral weight; "cuspidal analogue" of Dunn-Radziwill).
- **Lemma 1.3** (pointwise Type I, by Voronoi): T_f(a,X;v,u;W_K) << (X K N(v))^eps K^4 N(v)^{1/2} N(a)^{1/2}.
- **Theorem 1.4** (Type II, by the circle method):
  B_f(alpha, beta, X, v, u; W_K) << (X K N(v))^eps K^8 N(v)^4 ((AB)^{1/2} + A^{3/2} B^{1/4}) ||mu^2 alpha||_inf ||beta||_2.
- **Proposition 1.5** (average Type I, by Voronoi + cubic large sieve):
  A_f(alpha, X, v, u; W_K) << (X K N(v))^eps K^{14/3} N(v)^{17/6} (AX)^{1/3} ||mu^2 alpha||_2.
- **Section 3.2 (cubic Shimura lift; Flicker, Patterson Thm 3.4).** Bijection between even (odd) Hecke eigenforms h in
  L^2(Gamma_2\H^3, chi, tau) and even (odd) Hecke eigenforms g in L^2(Gamma\H^3, 1, 3 tau - 2), with
  N(nu^3)^{-1/2} lambda~_h(nu^3) = N(nu)^{-1/2} lambda_g(nu). **The only non-cuspidal Hecke eigenform in
  L^2(Gamma_2\H^3, chi) is the cubic theta function** theta_3 = Res_{s=4/3} E_3(w,s) (spectral parameter 4/3,
  Shimura correspondent: the constant function). All other eigenforms are Maass cusp forms with Re tau = 1 (no
  exceptional eigenvalues at this level). Hecke operators exist only for cubes T_{nu^3}; there is no "adequate
  Hecke theory" (coefficients at non-cubes are not multiplicative).
- **Lemma 3.1** (Weil bound, from Livné-Patterson Prop. 5.1 and Louvel (2.6)): |K_{Gamma',sigma,xi}(m,n,c)| <=
  2^{omega(c)} N((m,n,c)) N(c)^{1/2}; Remark: LP and Louvel state N((m,n,c)) where N((m,n,c))^{1/2} is expected.
  **Lemmas 3.2-3.3**: explicit cubic Kloosterman sums for Gamma_1(3) between the cusps I and E.
- **Theorem 4.1 / Cor 4.2** (Heath-Brown's cubic large sieve, one variable not squarefree):
  sum_{N(d) ~ M} | sum_{N(c) ~ N} mu^2(c) Psi_c (c/d)_3 |^2 << (MN)^eps M^{1/3} (M + N) ||mu^2 Psi||^2.

## 3. The method (from the sketch, section 1.2; the proofs were not read)
Vaughan's identity; Type I sums by a level-aspect Voronoi formula for twists of rho_f (automorphy of f), then a
bilinear form with Gauss sums and the cubic large sieve; Type II sums by Cauchy-Schwarz and the Browning-Vishe
delta-method circle method over Z[omega] to separate the convolution sum_b rho_f(a_1 b) conj(rho_f(a_2 b)), then
Voronoi on both variables. The author explains why Duke-Iwaniec's opening move (writing the coefficient as a sum of
Kloosterman sums via Poincaré series) "is not available for Maass forms", hence the circle method. Diagonal terms
are bounded with Rankin-Selberg on average because "no Deligne-type bound ... is known", "the author is not aware of
any non-trivial bound for rho_f(.) stronger than the bound implied by Rankin-Selberg", and "There is no
Waldspurger-type formula known for the coefficients of cubic metaplectic cusp forms".

## 4. Where it stops
Saving 1/34 (smooth) and 1/578 (sharp) only; Theorem 1.4 + Lemma 1.3 "barely misses primes" at level of
distribution X^{2/3}, overcome by Prop 1.5. No Kuznetsov formula, no spectral large sieve for metaplectic forms
(over the spectrum) appears in the parts read; the large sieve of the title is Heath-Brown's cubic (character) large
sieve.

## 5. What it changes in our record
- **N55, "a spectral large sieve on the cubic cover".** This 2024/2025 paper on cusp forms on the cubic cover uses
  the character large sieve, not a spectral one, and states that no pointwise bound beyond Rankin-Selberg and no
  Waldspurger-type formula is known for these coefficients. READER-7 found no spectral large sieve for the cubic
  cover in any source read or in searches (queries listed in the final report).
- **Lead's question (d).** Confirms in the level-Gamma_2 setting: theta is the whole residual spectrum, at
  tau = 4/3 (Laplace eigenvalue 8/9), and the cusp spectrum has no exceptional eigenvalues at that level.
