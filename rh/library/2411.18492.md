## 2. Main statements, with their numbers
- **Theorem 1.** `F(s) = sum_{j=1}^P c_j L_j(s)`, distinct Hecke L-functions of ideal-class characters of
  Q(sqrt(-D)), REAL coefficients c_j. Then `N_0(T) >> T log T` zeros of F on {1/2 + it, T <= t <= 2T}. In
  particular every Epstein zeta function of an integral positive definite binary form (fundamental discriminant)
  has a positive proportion of its zeros on the critical line. (The constant obtained in sec. 3 is c/P^3.)
- Background stated in sec. 1 (with sources): Davenport-Heilbronn and Voronin: many zeros off the line;
  Bombieri-Hejhal: almost all zeros on the line under GRH + Montgomery pair correlation for Hecke L-functions;
  `N_L(sigma_1, sigma_2, T) asymp T` for 1/2 < sigma_1 < sigma_2 <= a_L; Selberg (1999 MSRI talk) proved positive
  proportion on the line for linear combinations of degree-one L-functions (Davenport-Heilbronn function);
  Karatsuba (log T)^{1/2-eps} earlier.
- **Lemma 3 (label l00)** (Selberg's density theorem for L_j): `N_j(sigma, T) << T^{1 - c(sigma - 1/2)} log T`.
- Value distribution (eq. 8): `(log|L_j| - log|L_j'|)/sqrt((n_j + n_j') pi log log t)` is asymptotically Gaussian
  (n = 1 complex, 2 real characters); so at almost every height one L_j dominates the others "decisively".
- **Lemma 4 (l0), Theorem 2 (th0):** the mean-square estimates (5)-(7) for mollified L_j reduce to a bound
  `J(x, theta) << T/(theta log T)^{1/3}` (the "core of the work", sections 6, 8: not read).

## 3. The method in ten lines
Selberg's 1999 scheme: on the critical line F is (after the gamma factor) a real function; at most heights one
summand L_j dominates by a factor exp((log log T)^eps), persistently over windows of length H ~ A P^3/log T; on
those windows F inherits the sign changes of the mollified L_j (Hardy-Littlewood comparison of
`int |f|` with `|int f|`), which give >> T log T zeros. The new work is the mollified mean values for L-functions
of REAL class-group characters (products of two Dirichlet L-functions), which needs an additive divisor problem
with a main term.

## 4. What is assumed
Nothing (unconditional), as far as sections 1-4 show.

## 5. Where its frontier is
Positive proportion (constant ~ P^{-3}) on the line; not "almost all" (that remains conditional, Bombieri-Hejhal).

## 6. What it explicitly does not do
No statement for non-integral forms (generic z); no zeros off the line.

## 7. Facts in our own record that this paper corrects or shows to be already known
- **N45 (3)**: for a class-number > 1 CM point the zeros of E(z_D, .) are, unconditionally, a positive proportion
  on the line AND a positive density cT (out of ~ (2/pi) T log T) off it (Lee, `1204.6297.md`); conditionally
  (Bombieri-Hejhal) the off-line ones are a 0% proportion. So "zeros off the line" at such points means O(T) of
  T log T, a thin set; the record's phrasing ("zeros off the line") is right, the size is this.

## 8. Open questions the paper itself states
