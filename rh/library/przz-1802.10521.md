## 2. What it proves (parts read)
- **Theorem 1.3.** More than five-twelfths of the nontrivial zeros are on the line: κ > 0.417293, κ* ≥ 0.407511.
- **Theorem 4.1** (improving Pratt–Robles, Res. Number Theory 4 (2018), Thm 1.2). For ψ1, ψ2 of length N = T^θ, θ < 1,
  coefficients ≪ n^ε, α, β ≪ 1/L, Φ smooth on [1, 2]:
  I(α,β) = ∫ ζ(½+α+it)ζ(½+β−it) ψ1ψ̄2(½+it) Φ(t/T) dt = ΣΣ_{d,e ≤ N} (a_d b̄_e/[d,e]) ((d,e)^{α+β}/(d^α e^β))
  ∫ (ζ(1+α+β) + ζ(1−α−β)(2πde/(t(d,e)²))^{α+β}) Φ(t/T) dt + O(𝓔), with
  - 𝓔 = T^{3/20}N^{33/20} + N^{½}T^{½+ε} for general a_n ≪ n^ε (BCR) → θ < 17/33;
  - 𝓔 = T^ε(N^{11/6} + N^{11/12}T^{½}) for a_n = μ²(n)(μ ⋆ Λ_1^{⋆k1} ⋆ … ⋆ Λ_D^{⋆kD})(n) (Pratt–Robles) → θ < 6/11;
  - 𝓔 = T^ε(N^{7/4} + N^{7/8}T^{½}) for a_n = (μ ⋆ Λ_1^{⋆k1} ⋆ … ⋆ Λ_D^{⋆kD})(n) → **θ < 4/7**; D = 0 is Conrey's
    mollifier μ(n)P(log(y/n)/log y).
- **History stated in §1.3 (with the inputs):** Levinson θ = ½ − ε; BCH 9/17 "using Vaughan's identity on 1/ζ and
  Weil's bound for Kloosterman sums"; Conrey 4/7 "using results from Deshouillers and Iwaniec on exponential sums";
  Feng's two-piece mollifier and the gap reducing θ_F to 3/7, repaired to 6/11 by Pratt–Robles via Type I/II
  decomposition; Robles–Roy–Zaharescu (unpublished 2016) 48/95 from the DFI bilinear bound; BCR 17/33 from
  Bettin–Chandee. Bui–Conrey–Young two-piece mollifier (second piece of length T^{½−ε}) κ > 0.4105.

## 3. Method of Theorem 4.1 in ten lines
1. Start from Pratt–Robles §3.2.5: after the BCH/BCR analysis the error is the trilinear sum
   Σ_{0<|a|<A} ν(a) ΣΣ_{(n1,n2)=1} a_{dn1} r(n2) e(−a n̄1/n2).
2. Separate d and n1 inside the coefficient (factor n1 = h n1′ with h | d^∞; multiplicativity of μ ⋆ Λ's).
3. Write Λ_k as combinations of (log^{j}Λ ⋆ … ⋆ log^{j}Λ) (induction on Λ_{k+1} = log·Λ_k + Λ ⋆ Λ_k).
4. HEATH-BROWN's identity decomposes (μ ⋆ log^{j1}Λ ⋆ …)(n1) into Type I (one smooth long variable, the other ≤ W)
   and Type II (both variables in [W, U/W]) sums; W = U^{¼}.
5. Type I: summation by parts + WEIL's bound for incomplete Kloosterman sums: ≪ T^ε(AV^{3/2}W + AUV^{½}).
6. Type II: DESHOUILLERS–IWANIEC (Acta Arith. "Power mean values … II", Lemma 1; their variable ϱ is h here):
   a seven-term bound in E, A, U, V, h.
7. Balance at W = U^{¼}: 𝒜* ≪ (T^ε/d)(T^{½}N^{7/8} + N^{7/4}), hence N = T^{4/7−ε}.
8. The structure used is the CONVOLUTION structure of μ ⋆ Λ^{⋆k} (combinatorial identity ⇒ bilinear forms), plus
   "the additivity of the logarithm" to separate the smooth weight P(log(y/n)/log y).
9. Square-free restriction μ² would block completing the Kloosterman sums: that is why case 2 stops at 6/11.
10. General coefficients have no such decomposition: the trilinear Kloosterman-fraction bound (BCR) is all one has.

## 4. What is assumed
Theorems 1.3 and 4.1 unconditional. The ratios conjecture appears only as motivation (§1.2), not as an input.

## 5. Where it stops (as stated)
- Mollifier length 4/7 for every piece; "we are very far away from such lengths [θ = ∞]".
- §9 (3): "It is an interesting question to ask how one can increase the length of the Dirichlet polynomials without
  specializing the coefficients a_n too much." A commented-out paragraph in the source asks whether μ belongs to a
  wider class of coefficients reaching 17/33 < θ < 4/7.
- No barrier for θ is proved or claimed.

## 6. What it changes in our record
- Answers the brief's (a)(ii) in its cleanest printed form: for a_n = (μ ⋆ Λ_1^{⋆k1} ⋆ …)(n) (including Conrey's
  μ(n)P(log(y/n)/log y)) the largest θ in print is 4/7 (error T^ε(N^{7/4} + N^{7/8}T^{½})), input = Heath-Brown/Vaughan
  combinatorial decomposition + Weil (Type I) + Deshouillers–Iwaniec bilinear Kloosterman bound (Type II). No paper
  extending 4/7 for Möbius-type coefficients was found (one web search; not exhaustive).
- N57: the "known way through" the ½ point for this mean value is Type I/Type II + Kloosterman bounds (Weil, DI); no
  amplifier. See `conrey-1989.md` §6.
- IDEAS #34: PRZZ's §9 (3) poses, in print, the question "how much length without specialising the coefficients" —
  the general-vs-Möbius gap (17/33 vs 4/7) is an acknowledged open question, not a project finding.
