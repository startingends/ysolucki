# A. Reznikov, "Torus periods of automorphic functions and the meromorphic continuation of related Dirichlet series", arXiv 1008.0995 (source dated 30 Dec 2011; submitted to J. Number Theory)

Reader: READER-6, 2026-11-11. Read for ONE item of the brief: a precise modern statement of Hecke's theorem that the
expansion of E(z,s) at a CM point has Hecke L-functions with Grossencharacters as coefficients. The text after
`\end{document}` contains a referee-response letter and fragments of an earlier draft (the polar divisor was
corrected after the referee): status = revised submission; the final journal version was not compared.

## 2. Main statements, with their numbers (the paper's labels; sections are numbered, statements are not)
- **Proposition (Hecke) (label Hecke-Maass-prop), sec. 2.1.** E a quadratic field, T_E the corresponding torus in
  PGL_2 over Q, `E_s` the automorphic representation of the normalised Eisenstein series
  `E_s(z) = sum_{(c,d) != 0} y^s/|cz+d|^{2s}`. For `v = v_inf (x) v_f` with v_f the standard K_f-fixed vector and
  any Hecke character chi of E trivial on A_Q^x (unramified):
  `int_{T_E(Q)\T_E(A)} E_s(v,t) chi(t) dt = I_inf(s, chi_inf, v_inf) L(s, chi)`,
  `I_inf(s,chi_inf,v_inf) = int_{T_E(R)} v_inf(t) chi_inf(t) dt`. Proof: unfolding (refs Garrett's notes,
  Goldfeld's book); ramified chi add local factors I_nu and a partial L-function.
- **Eq. (E-L-expansion).** `E_s(v,e) = sum_{chi_n in X_un} L(s,chi_n) I_inf(s, chi_{n,inf}, v_inf)` (Plancherel on
  T_E(Q)\T_E(A)).
- **Eq. (spherical-exp) and the sentence after it (sec. 1.3), for E = Q(i), z = i:** every Maass form and the
  Eisenstein series expand as `phi(z) = sum_{n in 2Z} a_n F_tau(n,z)`, `F_tau(n,g) = <pi_tau(g) e_0, e_n>` the
  K-type matrix coefficients (eigenfunctions of Delta, `F(n, k_theta z) = e^{i n theta} F(n,z)`; Petersson for
  holomorphic forms, A. Good in general), and "the analogous expansion is valid for the Eisenstein series E(s,z)
  as well, and gives `a_n(E(s)) = L(s, chi_{-n})`", with `chi_n(a) = (a/|a|)^{4n}` the unramified Grossencharacters
  of Q(i) (the index convention between n in 2Z and the exponent 4n is the paper's; only K-types compatible with
  the unit group contribute). The normalisation of F_tau(n,.) is "the natural choice of the basis" given by
  unfolding (sec. 2.3.3); it is not written out as a formula.
- **Sec. 4.2 (General CM points).** For a CM point z of E: K_z = h^{-1} PSO(2) h, basis e_n^z = pi(h^{-1}) e_n,
  "the spherical expansion of phi centered at z", `phi(z') = sum a_n^z F_tau^z(n, z')`; for the Eisenstein series
  the coefficients are again the twisted torus periods of the Proposition.
- **Remark 2 (sec. 1.5).** For cusp forms the coefficients are related to L-functions only in absolute value:
  Waldspurger, `|a_n|^2 = L(1/2, BC_E(phi) (x) chi_n)/L(1, Ad phi)`.
- **Theorem 1.1.** `D_E(s,w) = L(s,chi_0) + sum_{n != 0} L(s,chi_n) |n|^{-w}` extends meromorphically to C^2;
  potential polar divisor in `s = 1`, `w = 1-j`, `w = 2-2s-j` (j >= 0), with actual poles at w = 1 and w = 2-2s.
  **Theorems 1.2, 1.3:** the analogous one-variable series for a Hecke-Maass cusp form (CM and hyperbolic periods)
  are entire after the factor `2^{w/2}/Gamma((1-w)/2)`. **Theorem (sec. 3)**: the value at the identity of the
  Eisenstein series (or cusp form) on the non-smooth test vector `|t|^{w-1} F_z(t)` is meromorphic in s, w, z.

## 3. The method in ten lines
Realise D_E as the value at e of the Eisenstein series on a test vector v_w whose K-type coefficients are |n|^{-w};
replace v_w by u_w, a function on the circle with singularity |theta|^{w-1} at the fixed point of a rational Borel
subgroup; all Hecke representatives gamma_i lie in that Borel, so u_w is an approximate eigenvector of the
group-algebra element `T'_p = p^{-1/2} sum gamma_i` (Lemma, sec. 2.4) with eigenvalue `p^{tau/2 - sigma} + p^{sigma - tau/2}`;
the automorphic functional is a genuine eigenvector of T_p with eigenvalue lambda_p; the difference gains one
order of smoothness, which continues the pairing one strip to the left (Riesz/Bernstein strip-by-strip
continuation). Poles occur where the two eigenvalues agree for all p.

## 4. What is assumed
Standard: smooth Eisenstein series (Bernstein-Kroetz, Lapid), Sobolev bounds for automorphic functionals
(Bernstein-Reznikov 2002).

## 5. Where its frontier is
Meromorphy in (s,w) of the double series; nothing on zeros in s, nothing on the variation of the point.

## 6. What it explicitly does not do
No explicit formula for F_tau(n,z) (hypergeometric/Legendre functions are mentioned only as "classical"); no
treatment of class number > 1 beyond the adelic statement (where the torus orbit consists of h Heegner points);
no functional equation in w ("It is not clear").

## 7. Facts in our own record that this paper corrects or shows to be already known
- **rh/NOTEBOOK.md N45 (2)**: "near omega only Taylor terms of weight divisible by the stabiliser survive; ...
  those coefficients are the L-functions of the Grossencharacters of K of matching infinity type. So the Taylor
  expansion of the family at omega is the generating function of the Hecke L-functions of K." KNOWN (Hecke;
  modern statement: this paper's Proposition (Hecke), eq. (E-L-expansion), eq. (spherical-exp) with
  `a_n(E(s)) = L(s, chi_{-n})`, and sec. 4.2 for a general CM point). Precise form: the expansion is in K_z-types
  (spherical, "Taylor-like") with fixed special functions F_tau(n,.) as basis, not a power-series Taylor expansion;
  the n-th coefficient is, up to a normalisation depending only on (s, n), the Hecke L-function of the
  unramified Grossencharacter of infinity type matching n; coefficients vanish unless n is compatible with the
  unit group (the stabiliser), as N45 (2) says. The double Dirichlet series sum_n L(s,chi_n)|n|^{-w} built from
  them is meromorphic (Thm 1.1): the "generating function" exists as an analytic object.
- What N45 (2) attributes to the elliptic fixed point (critical point of every E(., s) at omega) is the vanishing
  of the K-types not divisible by 6 (resp. 4 at i); at the other class-number-one CM points (not elliptic) the
  same expansion holds with K_z-types n divisible by 2 only (units +-1), so the first-order term is generally
  non-zero there (reader's remark from sec. 4.2 and the unit groups; L(s, chi) for chi of infinity type 2 exists).

## 8. Open questions the paper itself states
A functional equation in w; possible arithmetic meaning (special values) of D_E (Remark 6).
