THE QUESTION: name a mathematical structure, from ANY field, in which a sum LONGER than its family is controlled EXACTLY (not by an estimate) using a property that the Davenport-Heilbronn impostor lacks. For each proposal: the exact statement, what it gives on the near side of the wall, and the step at which the impostor fails.
