# Failure atlas

Separate from the idea log (owner, 2026-11-10). One entry per dead or stuck approach:
- **breaks**: the exact step that fails, stated as mathematics;
- **needs**: what would have to be true for it not to break;
- **supplies**: what the approach does deliver unconditionally.
Then the standing job, the Wiles move made deliberate: take the failures in pairs and ask
**"does what kills A supply what B was missing?"** Every pair examined is recorded at the end of this file with
a verdict (`no` / `maybe` / `yes`) and the reasoning; a `maybe` or `yes` becomes an entry in an idea log.
Written through `python3 tools/records.py atlas ...` (add / pair / todo / check).

## F1 · Look-alike approximants (wall W1)
- approach: Build functions F_N whose zeros are all real by construction (symmetrised finite Euler products, real phases cos Phi_X, Jensen polynomials at large shift, heat-flowed H_t with t > 0) and pass to the limit. Minimisers of truncated Weil forms were listed here too; they are real-rooted only CONDITIONALLY (see breaks).
- breaks: No proof that F_N -> xi locally uniformly without input about the zeros. Exact instance (lane C, COMPUTED, reproduced by its critic): F_X(t) = cos Phi_X(t), Phi_X = theta(t) + Im sum_{n<=X} Lambda(n) w(n) n^(-1/2-it)/log n + pole correction, is real-rooted for EVERY input, also for the Davenport-Heilbronn (DH) impostor: its off-line pair at height 85.70 is replaced by real zeros 85.262, 86.113 (X = 30), and at X = 1e6 by SIX real zeros (85.398 .. 86.001); min dPhi/dt = -24.6, the phase is non-monotone from X = 300. For zeta the error of the first 20 zeros is about 1.2/log^2 X (0.0069 at X = 1e6). Sharp truncations lose real-rootedness outright: symmetrised sum cut at 3 loses 1120 of 7975 zeros below 20000, because 1/sqrt2 + 1/sqrt3 = 1.28 > 1. CORRECTION (reader R-read-1, who read Connes-van Suijlekom arXiv 2511.23257 in full; digest rh/library/connes-vansuijlekom-real-zeros.md): for minimisers of window forms real-rootedness is NOT free. Their Theorem 6.1 needs the minimum of the spectrum to be a SIMPLE, ISOLATED eigenvalue in the WHOLE space (not only the even sector) with EVEN eigenfunction, the form defined from the distribution on [0, L] (Remark 4.3: delta'_0 gives a non-zero form whose symmetrisation is 0), and trigonometric polynomials a core; the sign of the eigenvalue plays no role. Appendix A (D = delta_0 + 2 pi b sin 2 pi x): every finite truncation has simple extreme eigenvalues (stated from numerics), yet in the limit the extremes 8/3 and -8/5 have multiplicity 2: simplicity at every finite stage need not survive the limit. So for this family the breaking step is twofold: simplicity/evenness of the ground state is an unproved hypothesis on each window (COMPUTED true for zeta at every L tried, and equally for DH below its L*), and convergence of fhat_min to Xi is wall WJ3.
- needs: A mode of convergence in which real-rootedness (or positivity) is closed AND which is controlled by primes alone; e.g. pointwise convergence of quadratic forms on test functions of bounded support, which involves finitely many primes.
- supplies: T3 (PROVED, classical): if A(s) = sum_{n<=N} c_n n^(-s), c_n real, has no zero in Re s > 1/2 and w >= (1/2) log N, then 2 Re[e^(iwt) A(1/2+it)] has only real zeros; finite Euler products satisfy the hypothesis for free. Stability margin min|Bohr lift| on the polydisc: 0.124, 0.043, 0.026 for products over p <= 3, 7, 13. The argument-principle count is kept by every member (20 zeros below 78.2 from X = 3 on). Reusable: free real-rootedness cannot tell zeta from DH; convergence fails exactly at the off-line zero (Hurwitz).
- source: rh/UNDERSTANDING.md U-3, U-4 (lead, COMPUTED + PROVED classical); rh/experiments/r1_C/PROGRESS.md E1 and its critic C3 (COMPUTED, two implementations); ideas #6, #7; wall W1 ; rh/library/connes-vansuijlekom-real-zeros.md (paper read in full by R-read-1; I read the digest, not the paper)

## F4 · Zero-side uniqueness at a first-failure window (wall W2, idea #8)
- approach: Suppose RH false. Take the first window L_0 with lambda(L_0) = 0 and a null vector psi; its zero-side equation says sum_gamma m_gamma psihat(gamma) e^(-i gamma x) = 0 on (-L_0, L_0); conclude psihat(gamma) = 0 at every zero and contradict the growth of an entire function of exponential type L_0.
- breaks: The exponentials e^(-i gamma x) over the zeros (N(T) ~ (T/2 pi) log T, infinite density) are over-complete in L^2 of every finite interval, so vanishing on (-L_0, L_0) forces no coefficient to vanish. What is true instead (PROVED, R2-parity Prop. 5c): sum_{gamma real} m |psihat(gamma)|^2 = -2 sum_{z off-line} m_z Re[psihat(z) conj psihat(conj z)] > 0, a balance, not a contradiction; for an even null vector and one quartet gamma_0: Re[psihat(gamma_0)^2] < 0. Every statement holds verbatim for the DH impostor, where psi exists: L_0 in (1.70, 1.72], crossing by 8e-30, eigenvector at L = 1.9 with 74% of its mass on the mode of frequency 84.3. A size argument cannot work either (N13): available weight sum_{n<=X} Lambda(n)/sqrt n ~ 2 sqrt X against log(T/2 pi) needed.
- needs: A contradiction from the PRIME side of the same equation, which has finitely many terms: for |x| < L_0, e^(x/2)<psi,e^(-y/2)> + e^(-x/2)<psi,e^(y/2)> - (log pi) psi(x) + int_0^inf (psi(x) e^(-2u)/u - w(u)[psi(x-u) + psi(x+u)]) du = sum_{2<=n<e^(2L_0)} Lambda(n) n^(-1/2) [psi(x - log n) + psi(x + log n)] with Q_{L_0} >= 0 must have no solution psi != 0, by an argument that uses Lambda(n) >= 0, support on prime powers, or integrality of the positions (it must fail for DH). Also open: end-point regularity psi(x) = O(1/|log(L_0 - |x|)|) (WP2), simplicity and parity of the first null vector.
- supplies: Theorem 3 (PROVED given Suzuki's Thm 1.1, 1.3, 1.4 and a form-domain assumption checked in part by the refuter; KNOWN in substance: Yoshida 1992, Suzuki p.5): RH false <=> a first L_0 with a null vector; a null vector at ANY window with Q_L >= 0 contradicts RH; lambda(L) is non-increasing (unconditional, Suzuki Cor. 1.2); {lambda = 0} is a closed interval. Null vector: real, even or odd, convex hull of support exactly [-L_0, L_0], psihat of exact type L_0 with O(r) zeros in |z| <= r, null space finite dimensional; N psi = <psi,q>p + <psi,p>q (for even psi: N_e psi = 2<c,psi>c). Strict positivity lambda(L) > 0 under RH (Jensen count against Selberg's c T log T distinct on-line zeros). Equality-case shape S3(iii): zero margin is the hypothesis.
- source: rh/KEY_LEMMA_SHAPE.md and NOTEBOOK N10, N13 (lead, HEURISTIC); rh/experiments/r2_parity/PROOF_first_failure.md (PROVED given cited theorems; checked by R2-parity-ref); DH numbers rh/experiments/r1_A (COMPUTED, reproduced by critic); idea #8

## F5 · Li coefficients: smooth part against prime part (idea #3, barrier B6)
- approach: lambda_n = sum_rho [1 - (1 - 1/rho)^n] >= 0 for all n (Li 1997). Split lambda_n = (n/2)(log n - 1 + gamma - log 2 pi) + oscillating prime part and bound the prime part.
- breaks: An off-line zero rho contributes 4 - 2(|w|^n + |w|^-n) cos(n arg w), w = 1 - 1/rho, |w| - 1 ~ (2 beta - 1)/|rho|^2. For DH's zero 0.808517 + 85.699348i: |w| = 0.99995800, the negative swing grows like exp(4.2e-5 n) and first beats (n/2) log n near n = 3.3e5 (COMPUTED + HEURISTIC, lead). So index n sees zeros only to height about sqrt n; for a zero of zeta just above 3e12 the first sign would be at n ~ 1e27. No bound on the prime part better than the trivial one was ever formulated: the required bound is the criterion itself (Bombieri-Lagarias 1999).
- needs: |prime part of lambda_n| <= (n/2)(log n - 1 + gamma - log 2 pi) for every n, proved with n as large as (height)^2 and without zeros; or an identity exhibiting lambda_n as a sum of squares.
- supplies: lambda_1 = 1 + gamma/2 - log 2 - (1/2) log pi = 0.0230957089661, lambda_2 = 0.0923457352280, lambda_3 = 0.2076389205543 (three independent routes, tests). The closed form of one quartet's contribution; the detection law index ~ height^2 (the shortest-sighted of all the tests on record). 2 lambda_1 = 2 + gamma - log 4 pi = 0.0461914 is also the Nyman-Beurling constant.
- source: docs/IDEAS.md #1 log and #3 (lead; COMPUTED for the first values, HEURISTIC for the crossing index; unreviewed); docs/KNOWLEDGE.md E3 (literature from memory)

## F6 · Window positivity through an estimate of the prime sum (wall WJ2, idea #2, barrier B3)
- approach: Prove Q_L >= 0 on L^2[-L, L] by estimating the prime sum sum_{n<=e^(2L)} Lambda(n) n^(-1/2) g(log n) against pole + archimedean terms, window by window.
- breaks: The lowest eigenvalue is mu_even(L) = kappa(L)(1 - chi_4(2 pi e^(2L))), kappa in [2.1, 6.8] over 165 orders of magnitude, chi_n = prolate eigenvalues; mu_odd ~ 1 - chi_6: 3.3e-2, 9.3e-7, 3.7e-15, 6.3e-30, 5.0e-55, 4.5e-97, 4.0e-167 at L = 0.25 .. 1.75 (COMPUTED, Arb midpoints, reproduced; L = 1.75 not converged), i.e. about exp(-4 pi e^(2L)). Any estimate must have error below that. Truncating the primes destroys positivity at once: Pick matrix from primes <= 1e5 has eigenvalue -6.6e-12 (lane C).
- needs: Krein's extension in constructive form: a positive measure on R, built from the primes <= e^(2L), the Gamma factor and the pole and from no zero, whose Fourier transform equals the Weil distribution on (-2L, 2L). Equivalently an identity Q_L(f) = sum of squares, exact to all orders.
- supplies: The exact finite stage: restricting the SUPPORT (not the sum) makes the explicit formula exact with primes <= e^(2L); closed-form matrix (digamma values + geometric sums, no quadrature), three independent implementations agreeing to 5e-60. The near-null ladder: f(u) = e^(u/2) sum_n phi(n e^u), phi = prolate functions with phi(0) = 0 (HEURISTIC mechanism, Connes-Consani), ladder ln eigenvalues -67.18, -52.05, -38.66, -27.14 against ln(1 - chi_n), n = 4, 8, 12, 16: -68.66, -52.89, -39.41, -27.63 at L = 1. Same ladder and same zero-reproduction for DH below its L*: none of it sees the Euler product.

## F7 · One-gap reduction (idea #20, wall WJ3)
- approach: With theta_L an explicit even trial vector, Davis-Kahan gives sin^2 angle(theta_L, f_min) <= (R(theta_L) - mu_1)/(mu_2 - mu_1); if this is o(e^(-(1+2 delta)L)/L) along L -> infinity then fhat_min -> Xi locally uniformly, Connes-van Suijlekom gives real zeros, Hurwitz gives RH.
- breaks: The hypothesis (GAP) gives mu_2(L) >= R(theta_L) >= 0 (R < 0 would refute RH outright): at most one negative square on those windows. By Theorem 4 (R2-parity, PROVED) off-line zeros cost one negative square in EACH parity block per quartet, so 'at most one negative square on every window' is already RH: the hypothesis contains the conclusion. DH: mu_1 = -0.71, mu_2 = -4e-17 at L = 2; negative index 1 + 1 from L = 1.74, 2 + 2 from L in (1.88, 1.90].
- needs: A lower bound mu_2(L) - mu_1(L) >= K L e^((1+2 delta)L) (R(theta_L) - mu_1(L)) whose proof does not pass through mu_2 >= 0; numerically mu_2/mu_1 ~ (32/15)(2 pi e^(2L))^2 (prolate law, 1.5e-26 against 6.3e-30 at L = 1), so it needs R(theta_L) accurate to exp(-4 pi e^(2L)) (wall WJ2).
- supplies: Theorem 4 (PROVED, zero side only, every step checked by R2-parity-ref; count known in substance): sup over windows of kappa_-(Q_L) = number of distinct zeros with Re > 1/2; in each parity block = number of quartets; attained for large L. Corollaries: 'at most one negative square on every window' <=> RH; one parity block suffices (Yoshida 1992 Prop. 1). The same integer is counted by negative eigenvalues of Pick matrices (Krein-Langer) and by critical values in the pencil.

## F8 · Signature target: N = primes - arch has one positive eigenvalue (wall WJ13 / WP1, idea #10 claim a)
- approach: Prove on every window that N has exactly one positive eigenvalue (Lorentzian signature), using that the kernel of N is >= 0 off the diagonal.
- breaks: It is implied by RH but weaker. Exact (PROVED, S1-parity B56, Schur complement, 0 failures in 20000 random cases): kappa_+(N_e) = kappa_-(Q_e) + [t_e > 1], kappa_+(N_o) = kappa_-(Q_o) - [t_o < -1], t_e = 2<cosh(x/2), Q_e^-1 cosh(x/2)>, t_o = 2<sinh(x/2), Q_o^-1 sinh(x/2)>. A one-quartet configuration with t_e < 1 and t_o < -1 keeps kappa_+(N) = 1; across an even first failure t_e jumps from +infinity to -infinity and N keeps one positive direction. Also vacuous for L <= 0.10: lambda_1 = -0.0742 at L = 0.10, N has no positive eigenvalue while Q > 0 (t_e = 0.842). JUDGE'S ADDITION (r2_judge 2.14, COMPUTED, 110 digits, two lattices): on surgery configurations of zeta with ONE quartet (17.578 + 0.3i) N has THREE positive eigenvalues (2 even, 1 odd), not one: c^T Q_e^-1 c lies between 5.8e5 and 7e53 there. So 'one positive direction of N allows one off-line quartet' describes what the inequality permits (t_e < 1 and t_o < -1), not anything observed; the Beurling counts 3 and 5 are 1 + kappa_-(Q). The inertia identities are machine-checked (FORMAL, board B142, B166).
